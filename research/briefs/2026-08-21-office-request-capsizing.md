Subject: Request for comment: August 20 post on the New York Harbor capsizing

To the press office of Rep. Nick Langworthy,

LangworthyWatch is preparing an entry on the Congressman's August 20, 2026 Facebook post concerning the August 8 capsizing near Liberty Island and the federal charges against Manuel Hernandez-Umana. We would like to include the office's response on three points:

1. The sworn SDNY complaint (26 MAG 3226) states that the tour operator's website and social media "do not appear to include any information about USCG licensure" and that the affiant is "not aware of the Tour Operator ever having applied for any USCG licensure." The Associated Press identifies the company as Zeus Luxury Rental Inc. Does the Congressman believe the company bears any responsibility, and has the office raised the matter with the Coast Guard?

2. The federal charge is misconduct or neglect of a ship officer under 18 U.S.C. § 1115, a negligence statute; no plea has been entered. The post describes the defendant as "reckless" and says he "must face justice." Does the office wish to clarify whether "reckless" refers to the NYPD's initial state charge or to the federal case?

3. Our review found the Congressman's Yea votes on the Coast Guard Authorization Act of 2025 (Roll Call 218) and the FY2026 NDAA (Roll Call 320), but no cosponsorship of passenger-vessel safety legislation in the 119th Congress and no statement from the office on illegal-charter enforcement. If the Congressman has taken any position on Coast Guard illegal-charter enforcement or passenger-vessel safety that we have missed, we would like to cite it.

We will publish the office's response in full alongside the entry. Our deadline for inclusion is [date + 3 business days].
