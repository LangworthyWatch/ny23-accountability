**Subject:** Request for comment: campaign funding sources and in-person town halls (LangworthyWatch, response by Oct. 19)

Dear Congressman Langworthy and staff,

I am writing from LangworthyWatch.org, a site that documents Rep. Langworthy's public statements and record using primary sources. We are preparing an entry on two subjects, and we want to include the Congressman's response before it is published.

**1. Campaign funding.** Based on Langworthy for Congress's eight FEC reports covering January 1, 2025 through September 30, 2026:

- Total contributions were $2,199,540.31. Of that, $1,201,226.65 (54.6%) came from PACs and other political committees, and $991,851.66 (45.1%) from individuals.
- We matched the ZIP code of each itemized individual donor to the Census Bureau's ZIP-to-congressional-district file. Donors in ZIP codes entirely inside NY-23 gave $194,775.25, which is 8.9% of contributions. Even counting ZIP codes split with a neighboring district, and assuming all unitemized gifts came from the district, the share is at most 17.3%.
- Donors who list their occupation or employer as lobbyist or government affairs gave $70,850. Of that, 84% came from Virginia and Washington, D.C.

We also note, as context, that the Congressman's 54.6% PAC share ranks seventh among 26 New York House members, and that several members of both parties rank higher.

**2. Constituent access.** In April 2025, the Congressman told WSKG that monthly telephone town halls are "a very successful and effective method of reaching all the constituents of NY-23." We have found no in-person public town hall since he took office in January 2023.

**Our questions:**

1. Does the Congressman dispute any of the figures above? If so, which, and on what basis?
2. Has he held any in-person public town hall since January 2023 that we have missed? If so, please send the date and location.
3. Does he plan to hold an in-person public town hall before the November 3 election?
4. Would his office publish a schedule and recordings of all telephone town halls, including any that were postponed or canceled?
5. Would he disclose his meetings with organizations whose PACs contribute to his campaign, or with their lobbyists?
6. Is there anything else the Congressman would like readers to know about how he stays in touch with constituents?

We will publish any response in full alongside the entry. If we receive it after publication, we will add it and note the date. To be included at publication, please reply by **Monday, October 19, 2026, at 5 p.m. Eastern**.

Our full method and the underlying FEC and Census files are available on request.
