Navigation Menu Navigation Menu News ▾ News Desk Sports Page FLX Life Weather Get Local ▾ Cayuga County Livingston County Ontario County Schuyler County Seneca County Steuben County Tompkins County Wayne County Yates County Now Streaming Advertise Events About Contact Langworthy, Blakeman plan five Southern Tier campaign stops Wednesday October 27, 2026 11:30 AM / Staff Report Republican gubernatorial nominee Bruce Blakeman is pledging to lift New York’s fracking ban as he plans a five-stop Southern Tier campaign tour with Rep. Nick Langworthy on Wednesday, Oct. 28. The “Fire Hochul” tour is scheduled to run from Owego to Lakewood, with stops in Elmira, Hornell and Olean. Blakeman’s campaign said lifting the ban and developing the region’s natural gas would drive economic growth, create good-paying jobs and attract investment to Upstate communities. Blakeman, the Nassau County executive, is challenging Gov. Kathy Hochul. Tour stops and rally entry times Blakeman’s campaign lists the first stop at 9:15 a.m. at the Owego Elks Club, 223 Front St. in Owego. The Elmira stop is listed for 10:40 a.m. at Elks Lodge No. 62, 300 E. Gray St. In Hornell, the rally notice advertises doors opening at 1 p.m. at VFW Mooney Post 2250, 245 Canisteo St. Blakeman’s campaign lists its stop there for 1:30 p.m. The Olean rally notice lists doors opening at 2:30 p.m. at Olean Elks Lodge, 209 W. State St. Blakeman’s campaign lists its stop for 2:40 p.m. The final stop is listed for 4:10 p.m. at American Legion Post 1286, 174 Chautauqua Ave. in Lakewood. The campaign notice describes the rallies as free and says public RSVPs are appreciated. The Hornell and Olean notices list 716-249-4842 as the public RSVP number. Media representatives are invited to RSVP to press@blakemanfornewyork.com. The campaign lists Madison Spanodemos as its press contact at 516-286-1539.
