Bruce Blakeman (Republican Party, Conservative Party, Vote Affordable Party) is running for election for Governor of New York. He is on the ballot on a joint ticket with Todd Hood in the general election on November 3, 2026.[source] He advanced from the Conservative Party primary on June 23, 2026. The Republican primary for this office on June 23, 2026, was canceled. The Conservative Party primary for this office on June 23, 2026, was canceled.

2014

See also: New York's 4th Congressional District elections, 2014

Blakeman ran in the 2014 election for the U.S. House to represent New York's 4th District. Blakeman won the Republican nomination and also ran uncontested for the Conservative and Independence Party nominations in the primary on June 24, 2014. He lost to Kathleen M. Rice (D) in the general election.[1] The general election took place on November 4, 2014.
