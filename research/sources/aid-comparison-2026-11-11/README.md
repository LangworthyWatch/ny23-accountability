# How much aid NY-23 gets compared with other areas (Nov 11, 2026)

## 1. State and federal aid to local governments, by county (Public Ledger data)
Source: thepublicledgers.org/deep-dives/ny-state-aid/, county tables FY2024 and FY2025 (retained here; the live CSV matches the repo copy byte for byte). Every row is marked "figures INDICATIVE; cite only the six in CLAIMS.md." The public-ledger CLAIMS.md (row at line 356) records that a NY-23 figure was "deliberately not carried as a claim (page is county-only; decision of the principal researcher)." The figures below are derived the same way the handoff did it, and they reproduce the handoff's $3,568. **They are not a reviewed claim.**

Population-weighted, NY-23's seven whole counties (Allegany, Cattaraugus, Chautauqua, Chemung, Schuyler, Steuben, Tioga; 495,240 residents, 2020 census). Erie County is excluded because the district holds only part of it.

| FY2024, $ per resident | NY-23 seven counties | 57 counties outside NYC | New York City |
|---|---|---|---|
| State aid | 3,568 | 2,498 | 2,224 |
| Federal aid | 982 | 747 | 1,327 |
| State + federal | 4,550 | 3,245 | 3,551 |
| Poverty rate | 15.7% | 11.0% | n/a |
| Full property value per resident | 78,311 | 158,166 | n/a |

FY2025 (preliminary): state $3,665 against $2,617; federal $856 against $612; combined $4,521 against $3,230; NYC combined $3,385.

State aid rank among the 57 counties, FY2024: Cattaraugus 1, Allegany 4, Steuben 7, Tioga 13, Chautauqua 14, Chemung 20, Schuyler 29 (Erie 39).
Reading: NY-23's counties have about half the property wealth per resident of the rest of upstate and more poverty, and they receive about 40% more combined aid per resident. That fits the deep dive's statewide finding that aid flows toward the counties with the least to tax. School aid is most of it.

## 2. Federal spending by congressional district (USAspending): not usable for a ranking
usaspending_ny_by_district.json (spending_by_geography, place of performance, district layer, FY2024 and FY2025).
- FY2024 New York totals with NO district attached: $70.9B in direct payments, $2.5B in grants (FY2025: $79.2B and $5.3B).
- Statewide programs book to the Albany district (NY-20 shows $9,533 per resident in grants), and the NYC districts show almost no direct payments. That is an artifact of how money is recorded, not a real difference.
- NY-23 for reference: FY2024 grants $262M ($337 per resident), direct payments $3.04B ($3,904), contracts $2.17B ($2,787). The contracts figure is procurement, not aid.
Conclusion: do not publish a district ranking from these files.

## 3. His statements
Searched: 149 press releases (Apr 2025 to Sep 2026, ../ec-record-2026-10/activity/press-releases/) and the transcripts in research/transcripts/.
