Here for Your Health

Fidelis Care offers quality, affordable health coverage for people of all ages and at all stages of life. We can help you find the plan that's right for you.

Important Changes Coming to Medicaid

Due to federal changes, some Medicaid members ages 19–64 will have new requirements to keep their health coverage starting January 1, 2027.
Learn more about these changes and how to prepare.

Open Enrollment Is Here

Explore our Qualified Health Plans featuring the 24/7 Telemedicine Program, preventive care, prescription coverage, wellness rewards, fitness reimbursement, and more. Enroll in 2027 coverage starting November 1.

Explore Benefits

Explore Medicare Plans

Enroll from October 15 - December 7 for 2027 Medicare Advantage coverage featuring benefits that may include $0 or low monthly premium, Wellcare Spendables debit card, and extra dental, vision, hearing, and prescription drug coverage.

View Plan Options

Dental Changes are Coming

Starting January 1, 2027, Liberty Dental Plan will manage dental benefits for Fidelis Care, Ambetter from Fidelis Care, and Wellcare By Fidelis Care members. Read about next steps and check if your dentist is participating in the network.

View Dental Changes

Essential Plan

With a $0 premium, the Essential Plan offers low-cost coverage, including free preventive care, for New Yorkers who don’t qualify for Medicaid or Child Health Plus. Coverage includes free dental and vision, plus low or no-cost prescriptions.

Explore Benefits

Renew Your Coverage

Coverage is no longer automatically renewed for members in Medicaid Managed Care, Essential Plan, Child Health Plus, or HealthierLife (HARP) plans. Members must renew their health insurance to avoid a gap in coverage.
Renew Today

Social Care Network

Members in our Medicaid Managed Care, HealthierLife (HARP), Fidelis Care at Home, and Wellcare Fidelis Dual Plus plans can access a network of providers to identify and address needs such as housing, food, transportation, and more.
