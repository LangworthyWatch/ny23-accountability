 Campaign encourages policymakers to lower prescription drug prices 
 
 
 
 
 Apr 13, 2021 - 02:35 PM 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 The Campaign for Sustainable Rx Pricing today launched a grassroots and digital advertising campaign &nbsp;urging policymakers to lower prescription drug prices. The campaign will target Washington, D.C., Arizona, Georgia, Iowa, Nevada, Alaska, Missouri, New York, West Virginia, Washington and Oregon. AHA is a founding member of CSRxP.
