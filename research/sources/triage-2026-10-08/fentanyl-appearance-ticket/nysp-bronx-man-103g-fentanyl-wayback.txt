 Pleasant Valley NY
 Bronx Man Arrested For Over 100 Grams Of Fentanyl
 PRESS RELEASE
On November 5, 2024, the New York State Police Community Stabilization Unit arrested a Bronx man for possession of a large amount of fentanyl.
At approximately 3:30 p.m., Troopers conducted a traffic stop of a 2018 Jeep Cherokee on the Taconic State Parkway in the town of Pleasant Valley for violations of the vehicle and traffic law.  Investigation determined the operator, Larrington G. Henry, age 33, of Bronx, NY, was in illegal possession of approximately 103 grams of fentanyl.
Larrington was charged with Criminal Possession of a Controlled Substance in the 3rd degree, and Criminal Possession of a Controlled Substance in the 3rd degree with Intent to Sell, both class B felonies.  He was issued an appearance ticket returnable to the town of Pleasant Valley Court on November 21, 2024, at 5:30 p.m.
