CONTINUATION OF PRIOR EXECUTIVE ORDERS
WHEREAS
, pursuant to Executive Order No.6 issued on October 8, 2021, Executive Order 170 “State Policy Concerning Immigrant Access to State Services” was extended;
WHEREAS
, amendments to Executive Order 170 also address ongoing issues and should be continued;
NOW, THEREFORE, I, KATHY HOCHUL
, Governor of the State of New York, by virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and laws of the State of New York, do hereby order that Executive Order 170.1 also remains in full force and effect until otherwise revoked, superseded, or modified.
G I V E N under my hand and the Privy Seal of
the State in the City of Albany this
16th day of January in the year two
thousand twenty-five.
BY THE GOVERNOR
Secretary to the Governor
