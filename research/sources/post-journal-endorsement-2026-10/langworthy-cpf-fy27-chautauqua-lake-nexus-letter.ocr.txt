Dear Chairman Cole and Ranking Member DeLauro:

I am requesting funding for the Chautauqua Lake Aquatic Ecosystem Restoration and Water Quality
Improvements, Feasibility Study. The entity to receive funding is US Army Corps of Engineers, Pittsburgh
District, W.S. Moorhead Federal Building, 1000 Liberty Avenue, Pittsburgh, PA 15222. This funding will be
used for remediating water quality and other hydrological impairments of Chautauqua Lake. The project is an
appropriate use of taxpayer funds because Chatauqua Lake is an impaired waterbody in accordance with the
Clean Water Act, with phosphorous concentrations that are three times in excess of Total Maximum Daily Load
guidance value.

The project has a federal nexus because the funding provided is for purposes described in Section 1201 (6) of
the America's Water Infrastructure Act of 2018 (PL 115-270). I certify that I have no financial interest in this
project, and neither does anyone in my immediate family.

Sincerely,

Nicholas A. Langworthy
Member of Congress
