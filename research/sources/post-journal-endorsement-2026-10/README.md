# Post-Journal / OBSERVER 2026 endorsement of Langworthy: source retention

Retained 2026-10-26 for a fact-check of the Jamestown Post-Journal editorial
"Langworthy has earned two more years in Congress" (Oct. 24, 2026) and the
Langworthy campaign graphic quoting it. All files fetched with
`curl -sL -A "Mozilla/5.0"` unless marked `.wayback.` (fetched from
web.archive.org because the live site returned 403) or `.ocr.txt` (tesseract
output of an image-only PDF). `.txt` beside a `.pdf` is `pdftotext -layout`.

`bulk/` holds the FEC bulk operating-expenditure zips/extracts (~850 MB) and is
git-ignored via `.gitignore` in this folder. Only the two per-committee
extracts (`C00817932_oppexp_24.txt`, `C00817932_oppexp_26.txt`) are kept in git.
