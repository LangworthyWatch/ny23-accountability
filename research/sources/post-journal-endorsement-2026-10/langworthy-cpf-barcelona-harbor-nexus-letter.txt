           Dear Chairwoman Granger and Ranking Member DeLauro:


          I am requesting funding for Barcelona Harbor in fiscal year 2024. The entity to receive funding
          for this project is U.S. Army corps of Engineers, Buffalo District, located at 1776 Niagra St.
          Buffalo, NY 14207.
          The funding would be used to conduct a feasibility study for breakwater repairs and dredging at
          Barcelona Harbor. The project is an appropriate use of taxpayer funds because it offers the only
          harbor of refuge along 47 miles of Lake Erie shoreline. The project has a federal nexus because
          the funding provided is authorized by the Rivers & Harbor Acts of 1936 and 1945. I certify that
          I have no financial interest in this project, and neither does anyone in my immediate family.
          Sincerely,




          Nicholas A. Langworthy
          Member of Congress
