Community Project Funding Requests FY24

The process for FY24 requests has been completed, for FY25 click here.

 

***

The projects I supported went through a rigorous review and consultation process and followed important criteria:

  • A demonstration of community support and local partnerships.
  • A demonstrated benefit to community, particularly underserved communities.
  • A clear plan to obligate funds awarded in a timely manner.

The projects I supported are listed below, each of which fell into one of the following categories:

  • Water infrastructure improvements.
  • Essential public facilities
  • Public safety
