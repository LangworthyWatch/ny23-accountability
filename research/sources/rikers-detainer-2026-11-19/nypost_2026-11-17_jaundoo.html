Illegal migrant accused of attempted murder at large after Rikers release despite ICE detainer: DHS

By
Louis Casiano, Fox News
Published Nov. 17, 2026, 3:42 a.m. ET

See more of our coverage in your search results.

Originally Published by:

  • Illegal immigrant trucker charged in crash that killed mom, 2 young sons heading to airport
  • Illegal immigrant arrested after dragging, striking ICE officer in Chicago suburb: DHS
  • Twice-deported illegal immigrant accused of trying to storm United cockpit, threatening bomb: DHS

A Guyanese citizen living in the United States illegally remains at large after being released from custody in New York City earlier this month, despite facing multiple violent felony charges and an active federal immigration detainer, according to the Department of Homeland Security (DHS).

DHS officials stated that Delon Raymond Jaundoo was arrested in Brooklyn on charges including attempted murder, first- and second-degree assault, criminal use of a firearm, and multiple counts of criminal possession of a loaded weapon.

His prior criminal record also includes an arrest for third-degree assault.

Advertisement

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) lodged an immigration detainer for Jaundoo on April 29, which the New York City Department of Correction declined to honor, allowing him to be released from Rikers Island.

Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin criticized New York’s sanctuary policies following the release, arguing that state and local laws prevent cooperation with federal immigration authorities even in cases involving violent offenses.

Jaundoo lawfully entered the country during the Obama administration but overstayed his authorized stay, which expired in January 2015, according to DHS.

Advertisement

“Despite these violent charges, sanctuary politicians in New York refused to honor ICE’s detainer and released this criminal back onto the streets to put more American lives in danger,” Mullin said in a statement. “Under Governor Kathy Hochul, New York has become a hotbed of illegal alien crime.”

“The sooner we put an end to these reckless sanctuary policies, the sooner we can all work together to make America safe again,” he added.

Jaundoo’s release comes amid ongoing restrictions on local cooperation with federal immigration enforcement.

Advertisement

Upon taking office, New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani signed an executive order limiting city coordination with ICE, and a state law signed by Gov. Kathy Hochul took effect in August barring local authorities from cooperating with federal immigration officials.

DHS stated that several other illegal immigrants arrested for or convicted of violent crimes, including murder, arson, assault, and rape, have similarly been released from custody under the state’s current policies.

“Governor Hochul has been clear: New York State is not and never will be a sanctuary for violent criminals,” a spokesperson for Hochul told Fox News Digital.

“By law, New York State always notifies federal immigration enforcement of any non-citizen serving a State prison sentence. Any assertion otherwise is a blatant mischaracterization of the facts meant to distract from the cruelty and violence we’ve seen from Secretary Mullin’s ICE agents.”

Fox News Digital has reached out to Mamdani’s office for comment.
