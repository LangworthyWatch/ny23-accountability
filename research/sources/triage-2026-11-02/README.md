## Checks
- "doubled the child tax credit": 2017 law (TCJA Sec. 11022, $1,000 to $2,000); P.L. 119-21 Sec. 70104 $2,000 to $2,200. Already MISLEADING (Oct 15 entry, Claim 4).
- "doubled the standard deduction": TCJA Sec. 11021 set $12,000 single / $24,000 joint for 2018-2025 (tcja-plaw-115publ97.txt); 2017 amounts $6,350 / $12,700 (IRS Rev. Proc. 2016-55, irs-rp-2016-55.txt). P.L. 119-21 Sec. 70102 raised to $15,750 / $31,500 (Oct 15 entry, CRS). Same pattern as the CTC: the doubling was 2017, five years before he took office.
- "no tax on Social Security for 82%": the White House/CEA figure is 88% (wh-2025-07-no-tax-on-ss.txt), with 64% already paying no tax under prior law; the law created a $6,000 senior deduction and did not amend Sec. 86. Claim 1 of the Oct 15 entry is FALSE; this is the fifth documented repeat (Dec 2025 release, Sept 9 town hall, Oct 6 debate, Oct 14 TV, Oct 30 reel). "82%" has no located source.
- "no tax on tips, no tax on overtime": deductions with caps and phaseouts, through 2028; already MISLEADING (Claim 6).
