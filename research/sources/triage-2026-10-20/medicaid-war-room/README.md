# Medicaid Fraud War Room post (official page, Oct 20, 2026): verification notes

Post (screenshot `post-2026-10-20-official-page.png`, "Congressman Nick Langworthy" official page):
"As a member of the Energy and Commerce Committee, I'm committed to rooting out waste, fraud, and abuse. In under a 90 day period, we took swift and decisive action against 50 high-risk Medicaid providers, halting over $203 million in potentially improper payments."

## Verified (retained text in this folder)
- CMS release, Jul 28, 2026 (`cms-war-room-release.txt`; archived https://web.archive.org/web/20261020231537/... playback OK):
  - War Room launched Apr 23; "established in coordination with the White House Task Force to Eliminate Fraud"; brings together "CMS, OIG, state Medicaid agencies, and federal law enforcement partners."
  - 42 OIG "Notices of Intent to Exclude" (~$160.7M) + 15 state enforcement actions (~$46.2M); 7 overlap -> 50 unique providers, "approximately $203.3 million in Medicaid payments since January 1, 2025."
  - Headline says "Stops"; body defines the $203.3M as payments since Jan 1, 2025 tied to those providers.
  - Release text has no mention of Congress, the Energy and Commerce Committee, or Langworthy (string search: Congress/Energy/Committee/Langworthy = 0).
- E&C post, Mar 5, 2026 (`ec-medicaid-fraud-investigation.txt`; Wayback playback FAILED, local copy only): investigation run by Chairs Guthrie, Joyce (O&I), Griffith (Health); letters to ten more states requesting program-integrity information.
- E&C Medicaid/Medicare fraud hearings (docs.house.gov event pages in research/sources/ec-record-2026-10/activity/docs-house/event-pages/): 118917 (Feb 3, 2026), 119065 (Mar 17, 2026, CMS's Kim Brandt), 119405 (Jun 25, 2026). All Subcommittee on Oversight and Investigations. House Clerk lists Langworthy on Energy, Environment, Health (not O&I). Whether he waived on to these hearings: NOT checked (no transcripts yet).
- CMS FY2025 improper payments fact sheet (`cms-fy2025-improper-payments.txt`; Wayback save FAILED, local copy only): Medicaid 6.12% / $37.39B (FY2024 5.09% / $31.10B); 77.17% from insufficient documentation, "generally not indicative of fraud or abuse"; "improper payment measurement is not a measure of fraud."
