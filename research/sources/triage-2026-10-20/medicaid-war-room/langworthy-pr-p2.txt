Congressman Langworthy Announces Nearly $13.6 Million in Federal Aviation Grants for Airports Across New York’s 23rd Congressional District September 14, 2026 Press Release Washington, D.C.—September 14 th … Congressman Nick Langworthy today announced $13,532,819 in federal Airport Improvement Program (AIP) grants from the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) for critical infrastructure improvements at three airports across New York’s 23rd Congressional District. Congressman Langworthy Announces $500,000 Federal Grant to Strengthen Hornell-Area Wastewater Infrastructure September 9, 2026 Press Release Washington, D.C.--September 9 th … Congressman Nick Langworthy today announced that the City of Hornell has been awarded $500,000 in federal funding from the Appalachian Regional Commission (ARC) to advance a major regional wastewater infrastructure project serving the Hornell area municipalities including Hornell, North Hornell, and Hornellsville. Congressman Langworthy Announces $76.2 Million Investment to Strengthen Rural Health Care September 4, 2026 Press Release Washington D.C.—September 3 rd … Congressman Nick Langworthy today announced a $76.2 million investment through New York’s Rural Community Health Integration Initiative to strengthen health care services and expand access for rural communities across the state, including seven counties in New York’s 23rd Congressional District. Congressman Langworthy Introduces American Quantum Competitiveness Act to Strengthen U.S. Leadership in Next Generation Technology August 31, 2026 Press Release Washington, D.C.—August 31st… Congressman Nick Langworthy today announced the introduction of the American Quantum Competitiveness Act, legislation designed to strengthen America’s position in the rapidly emerging quantum technology industry, protect critical domestic supply chains, and ensure the United States remains competitive with China and other foreign adversaries. Congressman Langworthy Announces $20 Million USDA Purchase of Concord Grape Juice Following Advocacy to Support Lake Erie Growers August 25, 2026 Press Release Washington, D.C.—August 25 th … Congressman Nick Langworthy today announced the U.S. Department of Agriculture’s decision to purchase $20 million worth of Concord grape juice, providing critical support to growers across the Lake Erie Grape Belt following months of advocacy urging USDA to intervene in a rapidly deteriorating market.
