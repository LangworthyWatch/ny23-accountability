I'm proud to secure $1.4 million for Chemung County to make critical sewer improvements necessary for the Southern Tier Logistics Business Park to continue the redevelopment of the former military depot. If we want to attract jobs and economic development, we need the public infrastructure in place to make it happen. This funding will pave the way for new private investments in manufacturing, warehousing, and logistics to strengthen the Southern Tier’s long-term economic future.
