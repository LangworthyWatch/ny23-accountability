                         ARC Projects Obligated in Fiscal Year 2025
         The Appalachian Regional Commission obligated funding for 640 projects in FY 2025 throughout the
                               13-state Appalachian Region. These projects include:
        • Nonhighway projects funded through the Appalachian states’ allocations;
        • Nonhighway projects funded directly with Commission headquarters monies;
        • Nonhighway projects funded through the Appalachian Regional Initiative for Stronger Economies
          (ARISE) Initiative;
        • Nonhighway projects funded through the Investments Supporting Partnerships In Recovery Ecosystems
          (INSPIRE) Initiative;
        • Nonhighway projects funded through the Partnerships for Opportunity and Workforce and Economic
          Revitalization (POWER) Initiative;
        • Nonhighway projects funded through the READY Appalachia Initiative, a Community Capacity Building
          Program;
        • Nonhighway projects funded through the Appalachian Regional Energy Hub Initiative; and
        • Access road projects funded either through state allocations or from the Highway Trust Fund.
