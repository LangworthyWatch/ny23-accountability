Our Work In New York
In partnership with the New York State Department of State, Division of Local Government Services, ARC is supporting 40 projects in New York totaling $7.8 million in fiscal year (FY) 2025. These investments have been matched by $6.8 million in New York. They are also training and educating 814 students and workers, and benefiting the one million residents in New York’s 14 Appalachian counties.

Investment Impact
In  FY 2025 , ARC and the New York State Department of State, Division of Local Government Services partnered on the following investments and projects.
Community Leaders & Capacity
$2.9M
invested in growing the capacity of local leaders, organizations and communities to address challenges and create opportunities in New York
Workforce Ecosystem
$2.7M
invested in Appalachian New York to expand community systems that will help Appalachians obtain a job, stay on the job and advance their careers
Regional Culture & Tourism
$850K
invested in bolstering economic opportunities from New York’s local history, culture and natural assets
Additional Priorities
$1.4M
invested to ensure access to reliable and affordable broadband, clean water and updated wastewater systems and highways and in entrepreneurship and business development to strengthen the economy in New York

(14 out of 62 counties) : Allegany, Broome, Cattaraugus, Chautauqua, Chemung, Chenango, Cortland, Delaware, Otsego, Schoharie*, Schuyler, Steuben, Tioga, and Tompkins
*Schoharie County is covered by two regional commissions but may only receive assistance from one commission in a given fiscal year. Schoharie County has elected not to be eligible for ARC assistance in FY 2026. If your proposed project includes Schoharie County, please coordinate with New York’s State Program Manager to discuss this matter further.
