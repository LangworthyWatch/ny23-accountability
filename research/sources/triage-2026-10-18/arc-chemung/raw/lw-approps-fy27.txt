Appropriations Requests FY27
The Office of Congressman Nick Langworthy is  no longer  accepting appropriations requests for Fiscal Year 2027.
The projects supported went through a rigorous review and consultation process and followed important criteria:
A demonstration of community support and local partnerships.
A demonstrated benefit to the community, particularly underserved communities.
A clear plan to obligate funds awarded in a timely manner.
