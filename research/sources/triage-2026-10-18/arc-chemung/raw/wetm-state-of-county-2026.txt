CHEMUNG COUNTY, N.Y. ( WETM ) – Members of the community gathered at the Clemens Center on Thursday for the 2026 State of the County Address to hear about the future of Chemung County.
The event took place on Thursday, March 26, at which time Chemung County Executive Chris Moss addressed attendees with updates on various projects taking place throughout the county.

Of the projects mentioned in the address, Moss highlighted the county’s focus on strengthening economic development by maintaining its manufacturing heritage. Specifically, Moss highlighted Siemens’s choice to place a location in Horseheads and the potential for an Amazon distribution center in Big Flats in the future.
Additionally, Moss noted that the county is working on improving its infrastructure to not only create more jobs but also create more affordable housing opportunities for residents. Infrastructure improvements for the county include sewer system consolidation, broadband expansion, airport upgrades and road improvements.

Other topics discussed during the address include the county’s efforts to enhance public safety and tourism through improvements that not only serve residents but also serve those who visit the area. More specifically, Moss highlighted the county’s investment in area parks, trails, and outdoor spaces, which encapsulate the area’s natural beauty and make it a desired place to live and visit. Additionally, Moss noted that the county is investing in enhanced public safety measures with the implementation of modern equipment, enhanced training opportunities and upgraded systems for first responders.
