TITLE: Langworthy Visits Lancaster Senior Center Ahead of Medicare Enrollment Deadline
DATE: November 21, 2023
URL: https://langworthy.house.gov/media/press-releases/langworthy-visits-lancaster-senior-center-ahead-medicare-enrollment-deadline

November 21, 2023 
Press Release

LANCASTER, N.Y. – Today, Congressman Nick Langworthy (NY-23) attended lunch at the Lancaster Senior Center, engaging with constituents and highlighting his office's services for seniors. With the approaching deadline for Medicare enrollment on December 7th, Congressman Langworthy addressed concerns and ensured they have access to the help they need to meet this deadline.
“I had a wonderful time at the Lancaster Senior Center today, where I had the honor of sharing some Thanksgiving blessings with our cherished seniors,” said Congressman Langworthy. “It was an important opportunity to discuss the upcoming Medicare enrollment deadline with them and address their concerns. They truly are the greatest generation, and it’s an honor to represent them in Congress.”
