TITLE: Statement from Congressman Nick Langworthy on President Biden’s Budget Proposal
DATE: March 9, 2023
URL: https://langworthy.house.gov/media/press-releases/statement-congressman-nick-langworthy-president-bidens-budget-proposal

March 9, 2023 
Press Release

“President Biden’s budget may as well have been released from Mars because it couldn’t be any more out of touch with the problems facing our nation. Inflation is the number one issue hurting every American, and the President’s budget that taxes trillions more, spends more and borrows more will only continue to drive prices higher and weaken our nation. This budget that adds an additional $19 trillion to the debt is irresponsible and a slap in the face to Americans who voted for a House Republican Majority that would force checks and balances on Democrats’ spending. The President proved today that he is not serious about fixing our nation’s problems or putting forth a realistic spending plan that can get through Congress.”– Congressman Nick Langworthy      
Congressman Nick Langworthy represents the 23rd Congressional District of New York which includes Allegany, Cattaraugus, Chautauqua, Chemung, Schuyler, and Steuben Counties and parts of Erie County. Visit langworthy.house.gov.
