TITLE: Congressman Nick Langworthy Announces $71,000 Federal Grant for the Fredonia Fire Department
DATE: July 17, 2024
URL: https://langworthy.house.gov/media/press-releases/congressman-nick-langworthy-announces-71000-federal-grant-fredonia-fire

July 17, 2024 
Press Release

WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, Congressman Nick Langworthy (NY-23) announced that the Fredonia Fire Department has been awarded an Assistance to Firefighters Grant from the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) in the amount of $71,094.90.
“I’m proud to announce this substantial FEMA grant for the Fredonia Fire Department, which will greatly enhance their capabilities and readiness to respond to emergencies,” said Congressman Langworthy. “This investment will provide essential support for advanced firefighting equipment and comprehensive training programs, ensuring our first responders can protect our community effectively.”
In January, Congressman Langworthy hosted a virtual seminar with FEMA officials to assist local fire departments in navigating the grant application process. 
According to FEMA, the primary goal of these grants is to enhance the safety and effectiveness of first responders while also improving their ability to respond to emergencies and disasters. They aim to address the evolving needs of first responders, from advanced equipment acquisition and updating aging vehicles to safety improvement and training programs.
