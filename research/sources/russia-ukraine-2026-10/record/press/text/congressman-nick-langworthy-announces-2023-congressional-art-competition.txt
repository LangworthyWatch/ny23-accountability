TITLE: Congressman Nick Langworthy Announces 2023 Congressional Art Competition
DATE: March 28, 2023
URL: https://langworthy.house.gov/media/press-releases/congressman-nick-langworthy-announces-2023-congressional-art-competition

March 28, 2023 
Press Release

WASHINGTON, DC – Today, Congressman Nick Langworthy is proud to announce the 2023 Congressional Art Competition for high school students across New York’s 23rd Congressional District.
On the competition, Congressman Langworthy said, “I am thrilled to be able to host this competition and showcase the creativity and talent of our young artists. The winner of this competition will have their work displayed at one of our nation’s most important buildings and visible to the millions of visitors who come to our Capitol from around the world. This is a great way for Congress to help foster the next generation of artists and I encourage everyone who is interested to submit a piece to my office.” 
Art can be submitted to any of Congressman Langworthy’s office locations across the 23rd Congressional District including Clarence, Jamestown, Olean, or Corning. Submission deadline is Wednesday April 19th, 2023.

The winning student will have their artwork displayed in Washington in an 11-month exhibit in the Cannon Tunnel and will also have an opportunity to travel to Washington for the Exhibition Opening Celebration of their artwork on June 20th, 2023. In addition, one runner up and four student finalists will have their artwork displayed in the Congressman’s office.
Artwork entered in the contest may be up to 28 inches by 28 inches, may be up to 4 inches in depth, and not weigh more than 15 pounds. For more competition guidelines, please visit https://langworthy.house.gov/services/art-competition.
Congressman Nick Langworthy represents the 23rd Congressional District of New York which includes Allegany, Cattaraugus, Chautauqua, Chemung, Schuyler, and Steuben Counties and parts of Erie County. Visit langworthy.house.gov.
