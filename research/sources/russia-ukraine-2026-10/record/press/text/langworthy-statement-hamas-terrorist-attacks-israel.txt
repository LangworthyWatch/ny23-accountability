TITLE: Langworthy Statement on Hamas Terrorist Attacks in Israel
DATE: October 7, 2023
URL: https://langworthy.house.gov/media/press-releases/langworthy-statement-hamas-terrorist-attacks-israel

October 7, 2023 
Press Release

WASHINGTON, D.C. – Congressman Nick Langworthy (NY-23) released the following statement:
"My deepest prayers are with the people of Israel," said Rep. Langworthy. "The unprovoked attacks and the brutal murder of civilians by Hamas Terrorists are horrifying and heartbreaking. I strongly and unequivocally stand with Israel."
