TITLE: Congressman Langworthy Introduces Resolution Calling for the Termination of U.S. Secret Service Director Kimberly Cheatle
DATE: July 22, 2024
URL: https://langworthy.house.gov/media/press-releases/congressman-langworthy-introduces-resolution-calling-termination-us-secret

July 22, 2024 
Press Release

Watch Langworthy's Oversight Remarks

WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, Congressman Nick Langworthy introduced a resolution demanding the termination of U.S. Secret Service Director Kimberly Cheatle. This follows the assassination attempt on President Donald Trump, the death of Corey Comperatore, and the injury of others on July 13th.
"U.S. Secret Service Director Kimberly Cheatle must be held accountable for the unacceptable failure to keep President Trump and rallygoers safe on July 13th," said Congressman Langworthy. "In our Oversight hearing today, she did nothing but dodge questions and deflect blame. Corey Comperatore is dead because of this failure of the Secret Service. The American people deserve transparency and competence from their leaders, especially those tasked with the critical responsibility of protecting our nation's highest office. She must be fired now.”
