TITLE: Rep. Langworthy Supports Bath VA Hospital and Other District VA Clinics on the House Floor
DATE: July 26, 2023

July 26, 2023 
Press Release

WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, Congressman Nick Langworthy (NY-23) took to the House Floor to support provisions in H.R. 4366, the FY23 Military Construction, Veterans Affairs, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, that will fully fund VA hospitals and clinics.

“I recently had the privilege of visiting the Bath VA Hospital, where I witnessed firsthand the incredible dedication of the VA staff and the challenges they face daily in providing the best possible care for our heroes,” said Rep. Langworthy. “By investing in these programs, we invest in the well-being of our veterans and their families and ensure they receive the highest standard of care they rightfully deserve.”
