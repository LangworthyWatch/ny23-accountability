July 25, 2023 
Press Release

WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, Congressman Nick Langworthy (NY-23) took to the House Floor to support provisions in H.R. 4366, the FY23 Military Construction, Veterans Affairs, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, that will fully fund veterans’ programs.

“We must never forget the debt of gratitude we owe to our nation’s heroes,” said Rep. Langworthy. “Our focus remains on preserving and strengthening programs that assist veterans in finding meaningful employment, educational opportunities, and mental health support.”
