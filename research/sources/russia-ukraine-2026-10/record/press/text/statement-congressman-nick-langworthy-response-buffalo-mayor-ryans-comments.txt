TITLE: Statement from Congressman Nick Langworthy in Response to Buffalo Mayor Ryan’s Comments
DATE: July 3, 2026
URL: https://langworthy.house.gov/media/press-releases/statement-congressman-nick-langworthy-response-buffalo-mayor-ryans-comments

July 3, 2026 
Press Release

"Sean Ryan can call me whatever he wants. I’m not backing down.

"I believe a city as great as Buffalo should proudly celebrate America’s 250th birthday. Instead, the Mayor canceled that celebration while presiding over a ceremony honoring a foreign flag. It was an enormous disappointment to the thousands upon thousands of Buffalo residents who were looking forward to celebrating this historic milestone with their families and neighbors.

"Those were his priorities. Mine are clear: I’ll always stand for America, celebrate our flag, honor our history, and never apologize for believing that America should come first.”
