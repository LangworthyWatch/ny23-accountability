TITLE: Statement from Congressman Nick Langworthy on the Chinese Spy Balloon
DATE: February 6, 2023
URL: https://langworthy.house.gov/media/press-releases/statement-congressman-nick-langworthy-chinese-spy-balloon

Image 

February 6, 2023 
Press Release

“Our military needs to shoot down or capture this Chinese spy balloon — the privacy and security of Americans are at risk.  We need a clear answer immediately from President Biden as to why we have seen no action to protect the American people. The administration’s weak and feckless foreign policy has led to the Chinese Communist Party violating our airspace and sovereignty. President Biden knew over a week ago about this spyware but remained silent. The lack of transparency and accountability is staggering.  This would not be taking place in the prior administration.” 
Congressman Nick Langworthy represents the 23rd Congressional District of New York which includes Allegany, Cattaraugus, Chautauqua, Chemung, Schuyler, and Steuben Counties and parts of Erie County. Visit langworthy.house.gov.
