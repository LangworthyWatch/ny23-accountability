WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, Congressman Nick Langworthy (NY-23) voted to pass the annual National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) for Fiscal Year 2024.
“This year’s NDAA makes historic progress to reinforce the strength of our military and protect our national security in the face of the Biden Administration’s efforts to reduce its capabilities and lull our country into complacency,” said Rep. Langworthy. “We cannot have peace through strength without staying alert and ensuring that our men and women in uniform—who make up the strongest military on the face of the earth—have the resources and support they deserve.”

Among its many provisions, the FY24 NDAA:
Authorizes a 5.2 percent pay increase for troops;
Includes a Parents’ Bill of Rights to ensure parents of children in DoD schools have the right to meet with teachers and review curriculum;
Protects and reinstates servicemembers who were discharged due to refusal of the COVID-19 vaccine;
Blocks the administration from reducing the size of the U.S. Navy and expands the capacity of U.S. nuclear forces;
Works to track and prevent Chinese surveillance within the homeland;
Reaffirms U.S. support of Taiwan and fully funds military exercises with our allies and partners in the Pacific; and
Encourages and enhances U.S. security cooperation with Israel.
