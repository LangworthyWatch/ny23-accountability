TITLE: Rep. Langworthy Supports VA Appropriations During Rule Debate

July 26, 2023 
Press Release

WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, Congressman Nick Langworthy (NY-23) debated in favor of H.R. 4366, the FY23 Military Construction, Veterans Affairs, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, which will fully fund veterans benefits and VA medical centers.

“Millions of men and women who have served this nation bravely have been failed by the Department of Veterans Affairs. Too often, the VA has left veterans and their loved ones with long delays, sub-standard service, and neglect,” said Rep. Langworthy. “I strongly support the underlying legislation before us today and hope my colleagues can overcome the partisan outrage to stand with our nation’s veterans.”
