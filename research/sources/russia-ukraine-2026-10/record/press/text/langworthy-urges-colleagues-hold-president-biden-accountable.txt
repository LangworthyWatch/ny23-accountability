July 18, 2023 
Press Release

WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, Congressman Nick Langworthy (NY-23) took to the House Floor to urge his colleagues in the U.S. House of Representatives to fulfill their constitutional duty to hold President Biden accountable.
“I want to remind my colleagues that it is our Constitutional duty to hold the President of the United States accountable,” said Rep. Langworthy. “The recent allegations of influence peddling and foreign bribery have cast a dark shadow over the integrity of our democracy. How can we trust a President to represent our best interests when he is in the pocket of foreign businessmen?”
