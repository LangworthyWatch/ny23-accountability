TITLE: Langworthy Condemns the Biden Administration’s Decision to Withhold Munitions from Israel
DATE: May 9, 2024
URL: https://langworthy.house.gov/media/press-releases/langworthy-condemns-biden-administrations-decision-withhold-munitions-israel

May 9, 2024 
Press Release

WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, Congressman Nick Langworthy (NY-23) released a statement following reports that the Biden Administration is withholding shipments to Israel:
“President Biden’s purposeful delay in sending munitions to Israel is brazenly subverting the will of Congress and an outright betrayal of our ally,”said Congressman Langworthy. “His actions are a gift to Hamas by weakening Israel’s ability to win and eroding our dwindling chances of getting the hostages home alive. President Biden must stop kowtowing to the terrorist-loving radicals in his Party and get Israel the help it needs to defeat evil, NOW.”
