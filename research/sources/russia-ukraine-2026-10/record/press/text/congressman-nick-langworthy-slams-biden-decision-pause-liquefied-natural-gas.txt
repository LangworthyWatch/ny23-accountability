TITLE: Congressman Nick Langworthy Slams Biden Decision to Pause Liquefied Natural Gas Exports
DATE: January 26, 2024
URL: https://langworthy.house.gov/media/press-releases/congressman-nick-langworthy-slams-biden-decision-pause-liquefied-natural-gas

January 26, 2024 
Press Release

WASHINGTON, D.C. – Congressman Nick Langworthy (NY-23) issued the following statement after President Biden’s announcement that he will be pausing all pending approvals of Liquefied Natural Gas exports:
“President Biden’s decision to pause Liquefied Natural Gas exports is a continuation of his America-last agenda to score points with the radical left at the expense of the American people,”said Congressman Langworthy.“This decision will cripple our economy by killing good-paying jobs, driving up home energy costs for hardworking families, and hurting American businesses. It also risks our national security and empowers our adversaries like China, Russia, and Iran. The President cannot continue to push out-of-touch Green New Deal policies that prioritize his own political interests over the well-being of our nation.”

Key Background:
Congressman Nick Langworthy has been a vocal supporter of American energy production and recently introduced the Energy Choice Act to block Democrat-run states from banning the use of natural gas and other safe, reliable forms of energy.
