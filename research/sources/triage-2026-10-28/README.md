### Posts 1-2: "Proud to deliver $1 MILLION" / "Our $1 million ... investment"

What the record shows (every item below is in a retained file in this folder):

1. **The City of Jamestown applied, and EPA picked it in a national competition.**
   FY2026 MAC competition, application deadline January 28, 2026 (EPA competition page).
   EPA selected 190 communities for 193 awards on **June 24, 2026** (EPA HQ release,
   `epa-2026-06-24-hq-270m-brownfields-release.txt`; program page). Jamestown appears in
   EPA's selections PDF as "Jamestown, NY | Multipurpose | Community-wide | $1,000,000 *"
   (`fy26-mac-rlf-supp-funding-selections.txt` line 110; the asterisk key reads
   "* Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act").
2. **EPA says the money comes from the 2021 infrastructure law.** EPA's own fact sheet
   (EPA-560-F-26-050, June 2026): "EPA has selected the City of Jamestown for a Brownfields
   Multipurpose Grant that will be funded by the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act."
   (`epa-factsheet-12967-jamestown-fy26-multipurpose.txt`). The java.epa.gov fact-sheet table
   row reads "Jamestown, City of | 2 | NY | Multipurpose | 1 | 2026 | IIJA | $1,000,000".
3. **He was not in Congress for that law.** H.R. 3684 passed the House Nov. 5, 2021 (Roll 369,
   motion to concur); NY-23's then-member Tom Reed voted Yea (`clerk-roll-2021-369-iija.xml`).
   Langworthy took office January 2023. (Precedent: entry 2025-12-infrastructure-credit, which
   already states he "was not in Congress when the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law passed.")
4. **The senators announced it four months earlier, and local press credited them.**
   Schumer/Gillibrand release June 24, 2026 ("SCHUMER, GILLIBRAND DELIVER $14.3 MILLION...";
   Jamestown row $1,000,000; "funded by the Bipartisan Infrastructure & Jobs Law")
   (`schumer-gillibrand-2026-06-24-14-3m-brownfields.txt`). Post-Journal June 25, 2026
   (Whittaker): "Jamestown is slated to receive $1 million, U.S. Sens. Chuck Schumer and
   U.S. Senator Kirsten Gillibrand announced Wednesday ... The money is from the Bipartisan
   Infrastructure & Jobs Law allocated through the Environmental Protection Agency's
   Brownfields Program." (`pj-2026-06-businesses-eyeing-brownfield.txt`). OBSERVER June 2026
   same text (`observer-2026-06-1m-brownfields.txt`). WRFA June 26, 2026 cites the senators'
   release and tags the story "Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act"
   (`wrfa-city-receives-1m-cleanup.txt`).
5. **No press release from his office at selection, or since.** His press-release index,
   pages 0-6 (Aug 25, 2025 through Oct 15, 2026; 70 releases), contains no Jamestown
   brownfields release (`langworthy-press-list-p*.html`; grep for jamestown/brownfield/epa/
   chadakoin finds only the Jamestown airport, stormwater, Randolph sewer and South Dayton
   EPA items). Compare his May 16, 2025 release "announces" (not "delivers") the CCIDA
   $500,000 EPA assessment grant (`langworthy-2025-05-500k-epa-ccida.txt`).
