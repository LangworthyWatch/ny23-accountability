Veterans Affairs facility in Bath hit by DOGE job cuts

BATH, N.Y. (WETM) — The Trump Administration's efforts to cut government spending and what it calls "bloat" in the number of people working at many federal government departments is now hitting the VA medical facility in Bath, according to a VA official.

What is DOGE, and what's it doing?

Lydia Delgado MHA, Public Affairs Officer at the VA Finger Lakes Healthcare System sent the following statement after 18 News inquired about job cuts there:

"A small number of probationary staff have been dismissed. This decision will have no negative effect on Veteran health care, benefits, or other services and will allow VA to focus more effectively on its core mission of serving Veterans, families, caregivers, and survivors. We cannot discuss specific personnel matters due to privacy concerns."

VA axes another 1,400 employees

The organization serves more than 33,000 veterans in its coverage area, which includes the Finger Lakes, the Southern Tier areas and beyond, according to its website. It has been providing services for more than 85 years. This includes outpatient clinics in Elmira and Wellsboro.
