# DOGE / administration cuts and cancer research, 2025–26 (retained 2026-09-26)

Question: how did the DOGE-era cuts affect cancer research, nationally and in Western New York. Assessment only; no entry built.
Existing coverage on the site: `2026-06-27-cystic-fibrosis-nih-credit-claim.md` (indirect-cost cap, ~16,000 applications
disrupted, FY2026 appropriations rejecting the 40% cut, his Rolls 145/190 votes).

## National record (all retained here)
- **GAO B-337203, Aug 5, 2025** (`gao-b-337203-*.txt`): HHS "violated the ICA when it withheld funds"; >1,800 NIH grants
  terminated Feb–June 2025 per HHS; NIH obligated "almost $8 billion less" Feb–June FY2025 than the same period of FY2024
  (62 percent of the prior-year pace); grant-review Federal Register notices halted Jan 21, 2025.
- **Sanders HELP minority report, Feb 2, 2026** (`sanders-2026-02-02-*.txt`): "NIH has terminated or frozen 116 cancer research
  grants ($273 million)"; also cites terminated ovarian-cancer and T-cell immunotherapy research. Partisan source; figures are
  the committee staff's count.
- **Science News, Nov 18, 2025** (`sciencenews_*.txt`): largest single NIH hit was $77 million in remaining funds frozen at
  Northwestern's Lurie Cancer Center.
- **NPR, Dec 9, 2025** (`npr_*2025_12_09*`): Harvard breast-cancer lab (Joan Brugge) NCI grant frozen April 2025; staff left.
- **CNBC, Sept 25, 2026** (`cnbc_*`): Aug 2025 NCI told the Pediatric Brain Tumor Consortium (est. 1999) it could not reapply;
  two trials shut, four transferred, "a 13-month delay during which no patients were able to enroll" (Dunkel); NCI says the
  transition to a larger network accelerates development and did not delay treatments.
- **NPR, Sept 24, 2026** (`npr_org_2026_09_24_*`): NIH ran ~44 days behind the 2021–24 awarding pace in FY2025; renewals 90+
  days late 3.9% (FY2025) and 2.7% (FY2026) vs 0.7% (FY2021–24); by July 2026 NIH had obligated roughly half its budget;
  Greater Bay Area Cancer Registry NCI funding $5.4M (2024) → $4.4M (2025) → $3.7M (2026); administration proposed a 41% NIH
  cut for 2026, Congress "did neither."
- **NCI payline**: Cancer Letter, July 25, 2025 (headline and lede retained; body paywalled): NCI R01 paylines "expected to drop
  to the 4th percentile," a historic low; FY2024 funded through the 10th.
- **NCI staff**: CBS, May 5, 2025 (~200 more NIH layoff notices incl. NCI; HHS: "fewer than 250"); Cancer Letter, May 23, 2025:
  NCI's communications team dissolved by RIF, "no one left to run the cancer.gov" site and the Cancer Information Service;
  STAT May 7, 2025 (headline retained; body paywalled).
- **Clinical trials**: JAMA Internal Medicine, Jan 2026 (PubMed 41247710, abstract retained) — cross-sectional count of trials
  with terminated NIH grants; Forbes' "383 trials / 74,311 patients" summary of it is NOT retained (403). Cite the study, not Forbes.
- **Indirect-cost cap**: CRS IN12516 — 15% cap policy Feb 7, 2025; permanently enjoined Apr 4, 2025; upheld by the First Circuit.
- **FY2026 outcome**: cancer.gov budget page — "The Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2026 (H.R. 1748) provides NCI with a total
  appropriation of $7.35 billion, an increase of $128 million compared to the FY25 enacted level" (cancer.gov's page writes
  "H.R. 1748"; the enacted vehicle was H.R. 7148). Cancer Letter Jan 23, 2026: NIH +$415M, NCI +$128M, indirect costs untouched,
  cap on share of grants forward-funded.

## Western New York
- WKBW, Feb 10, 2025 (`wkbw-2025-02-10-roswell-ub-passage.txt`): Roswell Park "has $62.1 million in active NIH grant funding";
  spokesperson: "A reduction in federal support of this magnitude would have devastating consequences."
- UB, Feb 12, 2025 (`buffalo_edu_*`): ~300 NIH projects, $81M/yr, ~40% indirect rate; cap would cost "at least $47 million
  ... over the next three to five years."
- Buffalo News editorial, Mar 2025: "NIH cuts will hurt UB, Roswell and the WNY economy" (headline; body paywalled).
