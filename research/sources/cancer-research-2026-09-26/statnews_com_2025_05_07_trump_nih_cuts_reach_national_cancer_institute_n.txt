The National Cancer Institute, 87 years old and burnished with a $7 billion annual budget and a mission to curb America’s second-leading cause of death, is the largest institute in the National Institutes of Health. But even the unique status of the NCI did not make it immune to the slashing and burning that has affected other federal health agencies.

Over the last several weeks, multiple sources told STAT, the NCI has started to face large-scale terminations and closures of key programs.
