We've been addressing a whole wave of harmful bills like AB 2030 in a number of states that
create impossible standards for dietary supplements from a number of directions.
Representative Nick Langworthy of New York's 23rd District introduced a bill in D.C. that
would block some of these misguided efforts.
It's called a preemption bill.
It would make federal dietary supplement standards the supreme law of the land.
That bill is going to get reintroduced next year.
Whether you've got a Democrat or a Republican as a representative, Americans can let their
members of Congress know they want federal preemption for dietary supplements, and that
will stop these problems before they start.
