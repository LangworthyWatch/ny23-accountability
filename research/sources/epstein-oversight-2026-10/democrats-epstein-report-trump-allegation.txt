                    Trump, according to Keul, promised to arrange her visa in the U.S. and ensure her
                    acceptance into New York University through his friend Paolo Zampolli, a close
                    Trump ally who was, until earlier this month, part of his Administration. At the same
                    event, she also spent at least 10 minutes talking to Epstein who she said claimed to
                    have organized “many parties with Don.”


                    Later that day, Keul alleges, Trump invited her to a hotel suite where Trump
                    “grabbed Keul from behind and forcibly raped her,” causing “bleeding and bruising
                    on multiple parts of her body,” the report says. Following the incident, Keul alleges
                    Trump made her promise she would keep quiet and repeated his promise for visa
                    and college admission.


                    Because Keul’s passport had been taken from her on her arrival, she was not able to
                    leave the contest following the incident, she alleges, according to the report. After
                    she returned to Switzerland, she declined to participate in an additional Trump-
                    sponsored pageant in Hawaii several weeks later.

                    TIME has been unable to independently verify details of Keul’s account.


                    In a statement, the White House denies the allegations and says that President
                    Trump has been “fully exonerated of anything related to Epstein.” “This ‘report’ is
                    nothing more than a desperate attempt by crazed Democrats to distract Americans
                    from the surging Republican momentum driven by President Trump’s historic
                    achievements,” White House spokeswoman Lauren Bis says.


                    Keul has previously spoken in public about her sexual assault allegations against
                    Trump, but the report provided new details of the alleged rape—as well as reviewing
                    similar accounts of other Trump accusers.


                    Read more: Trump Said He Banned Epstein From Mar-a-Lago. A Lawmaker Who
                    Reviewed Unredacted Files Says They Tell a Diﬀerent Story


                    In addition to Keul’s account, the report also compiled allegations from four other
                    women who have directly accused Trump of rape or sexual assault in connection
                    with the Epstein investigation. The allegations, spanning from the mid-1980s to the
                    1990s, had all been previously made public either through media interviews or FBI
                    testimony released as part of the Epstein files.


                                                                           Advertisement




                    The report’s release, just over a week before Election Day, comes as Democrats are
                    working to signal to voters how they will make use of their power if they gain control
                    of one or both chambers of Congress.


                    On Monday, Rep. Jamie Raskin of Maryland, the top Democrat of the House Judiciary
                    Committee, called on the Department of Justice to appoint an independent special
                    counsel to oversee the investigation of allegations mentioned in the report. So far,
                    Ghislaine Maxwell, a longtime Epstein confidant, is the only person to have been
                    convicted of sex trafficking of minors in connection with the Epstein case.


                    “An independent special counsel must investigate Epstein’s co-conspirators and
                    DOJ’s cover-up and ensure that state and foreign prosecutors have the evidence
                    they need to pursue justice,” Raskin said in a statement.



                    Allegations made against Paolo
                    Zampolli
                    The report also alleges that Zampolli, a former Trump administration official and a
                    close Trump ally, was “an active participant” in the Epstein sex trafficking
                    allegations.


                                                                           Advertisement




                    According to the report, which cites multiple FBI reports included in the Epstein
                    files, Zampolli arranged U.S. visas for young female models before they met Epstein.
                    One FBI report mentioned that Zampolli arranged an 18-year-old model’s visa before
                    she was recruited to give a massage at Epstein’s house, where Epstein allegedly
                    raped her.


                    Zampolli himself has also been accused of rape and sexual assault in a separate
                    case brought by model Victoria Drake, who alleges in an ongoing civil lawsuit that
                    Zampolli drugged and raped her in 2023.


                    TIME has reached out to Zampolli for comment.


                    Previously, Zampolli has claimed that he had introduced First Lady Melania Trump to
                    the President in 1998, but the report disputes that claim, citing two accounts—one
                    from an unnamed Epstein associate and the other from Amanda Ungaro, a Brazilian
                    model—saying that Epstein had introduced Melania Trump to Donald Trump.

                    The New York Times reported in March that Zampolli had asked a top official at
                    Immigration and Customs Enforcement last year to detain and deport Ungaro,
                    whom Zampolli had previously dated, amid a custody battle over their son. Zampolli
                    denied making such requests.


                    Until earlier this month, Zampolli held a diplomatic role under the State Department
                    as Special Representative for Global Partnerships. He was fired two weeks ago over
                    a months-long dispute with the department’s deputy secretary, according to
                    Politico.
