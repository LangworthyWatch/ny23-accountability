Department of Health (DOH)
The mission of the Pennsylvania Department of Health (DOH) is to promote healthy behaviors, prevent injury and disease, and to assure the safe delivery of quality health care for all people in Pennsylvania. Our goal is to create a healthy Pennsylvania for all.
