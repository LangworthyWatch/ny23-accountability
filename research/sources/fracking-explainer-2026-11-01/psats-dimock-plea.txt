Cabot Successor Accepts Plea, Funds Water Line Construction in Dimock Township 
Posted on
December 2, 2022
 | 
Atty. Gen. Josh Shapiro announced that Coterra Energy Inc., formerly Cabot Oil and Gas, has pleaded no contest to environmental crimes in northeast Pennsylvania. As part of the plea, Coterra will pay $16.29 million to construct a new public water supply in Dimock Township, Susquehanna County, and payment of 75 years of water bills for impacted homeowners. Private wells were contaminated with methane pollution due to drilling activities, including a drinking water well that exploded in January 2009. Residents were forced to travel for drinking water. Resources for Township & Municipal Officials
When elected or appointed, township officials automatically become PSATS members through their township's membership. If you're an elected or appointed township official in Pennsylvania and are unsure about your entity’s membership status with PSATS, your board secretary or manager can provide the necessary details.
PSATS offers its members exclusive access to training, resources, and events via the member portal at PSATS.org. If you have any questions about PSATS.org or your membership that aren't covered on our FAQ page, please submit a message, and we'll be happy to follow up with you.
