## Claims and proposed verdicts

### Energy (L4, L5, L7, L8, shot 08)
- **L5 "now you know my opponent supports [Hochul's natural gas and propane ban]": FALSE.**
  - Gies, in the same debate exchange (~22:37): "I will never support things like bans on wood-burning stoves or propane or no more hookups for natural gas."
  - What the post calls a "ban" is the All-Electric Buildings Act, which covers **new** buildings only.
  - The law is **suspended** under a Nov 18, 2025 stipulation in Mulhern Gas v. Mosley. The Second Circuit upheld it June 30, 2026, and the suspension runs until ~Dec 31, 2026 unless a cert petition is filed.
  - Same finding as Oct 8 triage post 2.
- **L7 "My opponent OPPOSES [the Energy Choice Act]": TRUE.**
  - Gies, 21:35: "I oppose Nick Langworthy's signature piece of legislation, the Energy Choice Act." The clip drops "This is why" and his stated reason.
  - Langworthy's line in the same clip, "He believes that you should be mandated to be in an all-electric home," is contradicted by Gies's "never support" line. The clip ends about 2s before that line.
  - Overall post: **MISLEADING**.
  - H.R. 3699 status since the Feb 2026 entry: no floor vote; 157 cosponsors.
- **L4 caption "Pennsylvania drills for natural gas... Kathy Hochul bans it": FALSE ATTRIBUTION.**
  - High-volume fracking was banned by DEC in 2015 and by statute in 2020, both under Cuomo.
  - Conventional drilling remains legal. DEC issued 52 well permits in 2024 and 58 in 2025, many in Cattaraugus.
  - "PA has cheaper energy" is TRUE. Statewide residential electricity: 26.39 vs 19.30 ¢/kWh (2025).
  - Comparing upstate utilities alone, NY-23's NYSEG, RG&E and National Grid run about 6-25% above PECO, PPL and FirstEnergy PA (Duquesne is higher than all three), not the statewide 37%.
  - That "her failed energy policies" cause the price gap: NOT SUPPORTED.
  - The audio is Blakeman, so his "more than half" line is not a Langworthy claim.
- **L8 "Hochul and Albany Democrats made their disastrous green new deal the law of the land": MISSING CONTEXT.**
  - The CLCPA was signed by Cuomo July 18, 2019.
  - Hochul's FY2027 budget loosened it: rules deadline moved to 2028; "to the maximum extent feasible and cost effective."
  - "Want to take it nationwide": NOT SUPPORTED.
  - "Electric snow blowers" quip: no mandate found (not rated).
- **Shot 08 E&C bills: MOSTLY TRUE.**
  - Langworthy's E&C seat is confirmed.
  - **POWER Act:** Guthrie discussion draft (Jul 22, 2026), not introduced.
  - **GRID Power Act (H.R. 1047):** passed the House 216-206, Langworthy Yea (cosponsor).
  - **State Energy Accountability Act (H.R. 3157):** Langworthy sponsor; reported, no floor vote. It requires states to "consider" a standard, so "exposes the cost" simplifies it.
  - None is law, and no committee or floor action on them this week.

### Hochul record (L1, L2, L6)
- **L6 "Hochul's public service commission has raised rates 48 times": NOT SUPPORTED.**
  - The number is Blakeman's campaign tally, with no published method.
  - His own Feb 11 release said 36.
- **L6 "Your electric bill has doubled": MOSTLY FALSE.** EIA NY residential price rose 36% from 2021 to 2025.
- **L6 caption "raised your taxes": MISLEADING.**
  - First-five-bracket income-tax rates were cut (~$1B/yr to 8.3M filers).
  - Top-bracket and corporate surcharge extensions apply to incomes over $2.1M and to corporations.
  - MTA payroll-tax increases do not apply in NY-23.
- **L6 caption "forced businesses and people to flee": MISSING CONTEXT.**
  - Census V2025, 2021-25: net domestic migration −719,579, shrinking each year; total NY population +167,082.
  - The seven whole NY-23 counties declined, mostly from deaths exceeding births.
- **L1 "Taxes have gone up $8 billion on your watch. You know where the 8 billion went to? Illegal migrants": MISLEADING / NOT SUPPORTED.**
  - The burned-in captions say "$8 billion"; Whisper heard "a billion."
  - At the Oct 13 debate Blakeman used two separate $8B figures, one for taxes and one for migrants. Langworthy joins them into cause and effect.
  - Comptroller tracker: $3.34B in state migrant spending through Sept 30, 2026.
- **L2 "Hochul treats illegal immigrants better than your family" / "luxury housing... a phone": MOSTLY FALSE, BLOCKED.**
  - **Hold: the speaker is unconfirmed** (failure mode 6).
  - NYC's debit-card pilot (~2,600 families) ended Jan 2025. No source found for "luxury" housing or phones provided by the state.

### Winter Storm Elliott (L5)
- **"We lost 69 lives in western New York": FALSE.** The official count is 47 (46 Erie, 1 Niagara; NYU Wagner report, Erie County ME). The source of 69 was not found.
- **"Those were the people that had baseboard electric heat only": NOT SUPPORTED.** No source ties deaths to a heating type; gas furnaces also fail without power.
- **"911 collapsed": MOSTLY TRUE.** 1,100-call backlog; 7 deaths linked to delayed EMS.
- **"Proposals in New York to ban wood heat": NOT SUPPORTED (hedged).**
  - The only bills found protect wood burning (S5391/A210). The 2022 Scoping Plan recommends no ban.
  - A full-text bill search was not possible because nysenate.gov blocks automated fetches.

### Tax / Social Security (shots 07, 09)
- **Shot 09 "3.6% COLA means $75 more a month": TRUE.** SSA announced Oct 14, 2026.
- **"Working Families Tax Cuts... eliminated taxes on Social Security for middle class seniors" + "$6,000 more relief": MISLEADING.**
  - The law does not change IRC §86 (CRS R48613).
  - The $6,000 is a deduction for everyone 65+ in tax years 2025-28. It is worth $600-$1,320 per person at 10-22% brackets and $0 to non-filers.
  - About 20% of beneficiaries are under 65 and get nothing.
  - Same core as entries 2025-12-social-security-tax and 2026-09-09 (both MISLEADING). What's new: the deduction is placed beside the cash COLA figure as though both were dollars in hand.
- **Shot 07 tax list: MOSTLY TRUE / MISSING CONTEXT.**
  - Tips and overtime: capped deductions through 2028; overtime covers the premium only.
  - CTC: $2,000 to $2,200, SSN requirements.
  - Senior relief and standard deduction: TRUE as worded.

### Celiac Safety Act (L9, shot 05)
- **"I'm proud to... cosponsor the bipartisan Celiac Safety Act": NOT SUPPORTED as of Oct 15.**
  - H.R. 9048 (Cleaver D-MO), 51 cosponsors (44 D, 7 R). It is genuinely bipartisan.
  - Langworthy is not in BILLSTATUS (last updated Sept 9; independently re-checked Oct 15), not in the congress.gov API, and not in the Congressional Record "Additional Sponsors" for Oct 6, 9 or 13.
  - He doesn't claim cosponsorship on air.
  - **Re-check congress.gov and the next Record before publishing.** If he is added, the verdict is TRUE.
  - The bill adds gluten grains to the major-allergen list; it does not set "gluten-free" standards as his on-air framing implies.
