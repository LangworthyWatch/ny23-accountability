Reproducible SoQL queries against data.ny.gov dataset szye-wmt3 (NYS DEC Oil, Gas & Other Regulated Wells), run 2026-10-15.
Well types: OD = oil development, GD = gas development, GE = gas extension, GW = gas wildcat (DEC well-type codes).
Endpoint: https://data.ny.gov/resource/szye-wmt3.json
