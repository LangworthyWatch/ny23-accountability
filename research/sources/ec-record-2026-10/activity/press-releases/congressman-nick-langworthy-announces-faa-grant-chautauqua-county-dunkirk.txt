Congressman Nick Langworthy Announces FAA Grant for Chautauqua County Dunkirk Airport
August 25, 2025 
Press Release
WASHINGTON, D.C. – 
Today, Congressman Nick Langworthy (NY-23) announced the Federal Aviation Administration has awarded $48,011 to the Chautauqua County/Dunkirk Airport (DKK) for the Phase 1 design work to reconstruct three existing gates that have reached the end of their useful lives. 
Congressman Langworthy said, 
“I am proud to see this money invested in maintaining our aviation infrastructure here in Chautauqua County. As chairman of the Aviation Safety Caucus, I am committed to keeping our air travel safe on the tarmac and in the skies, and I will always fight for infrastructure that delivers results for our rural communities.”
