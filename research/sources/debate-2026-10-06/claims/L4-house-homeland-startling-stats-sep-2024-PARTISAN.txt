• Since the start of Fiscal Year (FY) 2021 in October 2020, Customs and Border Protection (CBP) has recorded
  more than 10.8 million encounters nationwide, including more than 8.72 million at the Southwest border.
• By contrast, CBP recorded around 3 million encounters nationwide, including 2.37 million at the SWB, from
  FY2017-2020.
• SWB encounters in FY2024 (2.13M) increased 23% compared to FY2021. Nationwide border encounters
  this fiscal year increased nearly 50% compared to FY2021.
• Of the 2.9 million nationwide encounters in FY2024, 46% were at ports of entry, while only 15% of
  nationwide encounters were at ports of entry in FY021.
