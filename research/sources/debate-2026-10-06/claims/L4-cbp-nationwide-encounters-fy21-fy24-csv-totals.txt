Interpretation notes: (1) Title 42 expulsions (2.75M) were immediate expulsions, not entries; (2) OFO encounters (3.33M) occurred at ports of entry (airports, seaports, land ports), the majority of which in FY2023-24 were CBP One scheduled appointments and other inspections; (3) encounters count events, and CBP has reported significant repeat crossings (recidivism) under Title 42; (4) the dataset does not count "gotaways" (crossers not encountered).
