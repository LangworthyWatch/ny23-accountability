BATAVIA, N.Y. — U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement says it plans to add 100 detention beds at its federally operated facility in Batavia, directly linking the expansion to a new state law restricting local cooperation with federal immigration authorities.
ICE told Spectrum News 1 it is constructing a new hard-sided detention facility on federal property at the Buffalo Federal Detention Facility.
“Due to New York State laws prohibiting counties from contracting with ICE to house illegal aliens, ICE is constructing a new hard-sided detention facility on federal property at the Buffalo Federal Detention Facility in Batavia, New York, to ensure sufficient detention capacity in support of the agency’s immigration enforcement mission,” ICE said in a statement.
The agency said the new facility will have 100 beds and operate under ICE’s national detention standards.
