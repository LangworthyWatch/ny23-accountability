VERBATIM PASSAGE(S):
This dashboard captures the projected 1-year human impact of funding changes for aid and support organizations. These numbers have been frozen at the one-year horizon from when cuts began.
A Note on the Retirement of the Impact Counter Dashboard
After months of tracking the ripple effects of funding cuts — beginning with the sudden and unplanned cuts to PEPFAR and expanding to capture the broader human cost of foreign aid reductions — we are retiring the Impact Counter Dashboard.
This project was never meant to be permanent. Our models were built to project impact over the course of a year, and we've now passed that horizon. Rather than take the dashboard down entirely, we will be freezing the numbers at the one-year mark from when the cuts began.
