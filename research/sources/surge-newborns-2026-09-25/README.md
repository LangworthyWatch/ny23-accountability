## Where the bill comes from
- **Surge to Save Newborns coalition** (`prnewswire_surge_coalition_launch.txt`, Mar 5, 2026): "Founded by more than 15 rare
  disease advocacy organizations, like the ALD Alliance and Hunter's Hope Foundation, along with support from Travere
  Therapeutics"; site footer: "supported by Travere Therapeutics and BioMarin" (`surgetosave_org_.txt`). Manatt Health study
  "prepared with support from Travere Therapeutics" (`manatt-every-newborn-every-state-2026-03-04.txt`): $173,387,316 to implement
  all RUSP conditions plus two anticipated additions across states ($5.14/sample, $6.95/newborn); **New York's share $7,609,999**;
  Texas $24.2M. The coalition's ask is a one-time surge; the bill as described is $35M/yr × 5 = $175M. Manatt: "Until 2025, the
  RUSP was formulated based on the recommendations of the Advisory Committee on Heritable Disorders in Newborns and Children."
  Hunter's Hope's Anna Grantham is a founding partner. Coalition, not accusation: industry support is disclosed by the coalition itself.
- **H.R. 4709** (Newborn Screening Saves Lives Reauthorization Act, Morrison/Schrier/Simpson/Langworthy co-leads; Schrier release
  July 25, 2025, `schrier_newborn_release.txt`, Langworthy: "I'm proud to co-lead ... This reauthorization will undoubtedly save
  lives"). Forwarded by the E&C Health Subcommittee (his subcommittee) Sept 10, 2025; **no full-committee action in the 12 months since**.

## Accountability angles (for an entry)
1. The panel this bill funds states to implement has no expert committee behind it: HHS terminated the ACHDNC April 1, 2025;
   HHS FR notices Aug 14 and Dec 22, 2025 say so; replacement is a $700,000 APHL "stakeholder workgroup" (FR Aug 12, 2026).
   He thanked the committee by name in July 2024 and did not mention its abolition today. Kelly said they are seeking a meeting with Kennedy.
2. His own reauthorization bill (H.R. 4709), which restores the committee's statutory footing, has been parked in his committee
   for a year while a new bill is announced.
3. Same month record: Yea on P.L. 119-21, Rolls 145 (May 22, 2025) and 190 (July 3, 2025) (−$911B Medicaid; Medicaid pays for ~4 in 10 births and nearly half of rural births, KFF).
