## What was wrong
- The May 22 entry said the letter was "sent after the purchase deal was announced" and "after it was over." It was sent five days before Ridglan confirmed the sale (and 178 days after the October 28, 2025 settlement).
- The May 5 entry dated the first departure May 2 and placed it "in Marshall, WI." It was May 1. Location sources conflict: his release and WMTV's April 29 story say Blue Mounds; WMTV's May 1 dateline and Fox say Marshall. The location was removed.
- The May 22 entry and its index row gave May 1 as the start of the posts; the first documented post is May 3.
