(WMTV) - Ridglan Farms confirmed Wednesday it reached an agreement to sell a majority of their dogs to different groups. Ridglan Farms said the decision to sell “a substantial majority of its dogs” came after a series of negotiations with groups that agreed to buy the animals. Big Dog Ranch Rescue and the Center for a Humane economy explained on the Big Dog Ranch Rescue’s website that it planned to buy 1,000 dogs from the Town of Blue Mounds facility. As of 5:10 p.m., that webpage has since been taken down. Big Dog Ranch Rescue had said on its page that it was asking for donations to help support medical care and rehabilitation for the dogs. Big Dog Ranch Rescue and the Center for a Humane Economy and Animal Wellness Action said they will make an announcement about the fate of beagles housed at Ridglan Farms during a press conference at 11 a.m. Thursday. It is unclear when the transfer will happen or how much money Ridglan Farms agreed to sell the dogs for. Also on Wednesday, one of four people accused of breaking into Ridglan Farms in March appeared in Dane County Court for a preliminary hearing. Court Commissioner Brian Asmus found probable cause and approved a bindover, meaning that the case against Wayne Hsiung can move forward to trial.
