# Children's health care under the Trump administration, 2025–26: sources retained 2026-09-25

Trigger: Langworthy official-page teaser (Sept 25, 2026, ~9:30 AM) — "BIG ANNOUNCEMENT TODAY ... joined by Buffalo Bills legend
Jim Kelly and his wife Jill to share major news about our fight to save newborn babies' lives." Announcement not yet posted
when the page was read at ~11:40 AM (newest post was the COMPACT Act beagle-testing item). Question asked: how has the
administration affected children's health care?

## 1. Newborn screening (the announcement's likely subject)
- Langworthy's own record (retained corpus, `../triage-2026-09-23/press/`): July 9, 2024 release celebrating HHS's final
  approval of Krabbe disease on the RUSP, thanking "the Advisory Committee on Heritable Disorders in Newborns and Children
  for their thorough review and recommendation"; May 2023 letter to Becerra after the committee's tie vote.
- **HHS terminated that committee (ACHDNC) on April 1, 2025** (CRS R48757, `everycrsreport_com_reports_R48757_html.txt`:
  "the committee's termination on April 1, 2025"; PPMD Apr 4, 2025: "terminated, effective immediately"; STAT Apr 24, 2025).
  HHS's own Federal Register notices confirm it: Aug 14, 2025 DMD/MLD comment notices ("ACHDNC, now terminated");
  Dec 22, 2025 additions of Duchenne and MLD to the RUSP by the Secretary directly ("prior to issuing a recommendation to the
  Secretary on the inclusion of DMD to the RUSP, ACHDNC was terminated") — `fr_2025-15433.txt`, `fr_2025-15432.txt`,
  `fr_2025-23573.txt`, `fr_2025-23574.txt`. HRSA's committee web pages (`hrsa-*.txt`) were last reviewed Aug 2025 / Nov 2024
  and never say "terminated."
- **Replacement pathway:** FR Aug 12, 2026 (`fr_2026-16396.txt`): HRSA gives the Association of Public Health Laboratories a
  $700,000 non-competitive supplement to convene a "National Newborn Screening Stakeholder Workgroup" as "a pathway for
  conditions to be considered and recommended for inclusion in the RUSP"; the workgroup reports to HRSA, HRSA recommends to the
  Secretary, "who has final decision-making authority." Not a FACA committee; no statutory 180-day decision clock.
- **H.R. 4709, Newborn Screening Saves Lives Reauthorization Act of 2025** (`BILLSTATUS-119hr4709.xml`, `BILLS-119hr4709ih.htm`):
  introduced July 23, 2025 by Rep. Kelly Morrison (D-MN); **Langworthy is an original cosponsor (July 23, 2025)**; 7 cosponsors;
  E&C Health Subcommittee forwarded it by voice vote Sept 10, 2025; **no action since** (BILLSTATUS updated Sept 19, 2026).
  Reauthorizes newborn-screening programs through FY2030, makes the Hunter Kelly Research Program mandatory ("may" → "shall"),
  raises the sec. 1117 authorization from $11.9M to $20.883M, and rewrites CDC surveillance to run "taking into consideration the
  expertise of the Advisory Committee on Heritable Disorders in Newborns and Children established under section 1111" — i.e.,
  it presumes the committee HHS abolished. Prior authorization lapsed in 2019.

## 2. Medicaid / CHIP / SNAP (the OBBBA, P.L. 119-21; Langworthy voted Yea on Roll 145 (May 22, 2025) and Aye on Roll 190 (July 3, 2025) — see existing entries)
- KFF Oct 15, 2025 (`kff-2025-10-15-*.txt`): nearly 4 in 10 U.S. children are on Medicaid; CBO: H.R. 1 raises the uninsured by
  10 million by 2034; federal Medicaid spending −$911B and SNAP −$187B over ten years, "an estimated 1 million children with
  reduced or eliminated food assistance"; CMS "restricted Medicaid waivers for multi-year continuous eligibility for Medicaid
  and CHIP children" (12 states); immigrant eligibility narrowed; CBO expects the law "will redistribute wealth from the lowest
  income families to the highest incomes."
- CCF July 22, 2025 explainer (`ccf_georgetown_edu_2025_07_22_*.txt`): CBO Aug 11, 2025 — Medicaid/CHIP provisions account for
  7.5 million of the 10 million; work reporting for expansion adults starts Jan 1, 2027; retroactive coverage cut; eligibility
  for many lawfully present immigrants ended; CHIP-specific protections from the 2023–24 rule retained.
- CCF May 28, 2026 (`ccf_georgetown_edu_2026_05_28_*.txt`): "2 million fewer children were enrolled in Medicaid and CHIP as of
  April 2026, as compared to January 2025"; CMS's own data show a 4% (1.5M) child decline in year one; CBO May 2026 slides
  (cbo.gov 62380, curl-blocked, cited via CCF) project child Medicaid enrollment down 3 million 2026–2036.
- KFF Health News June 5, 2026: "More than 1 million children have lost insurance since President Donald Trump took office."
- CNBC Aug 11, 2026: rule ending Medicaid/CHIP funding for gender-affirming care for minors.
- NOT FOUND: a New York or NY-23 child-enrollment figure. CCF's tracker is a Datawrapper chart (workbook holds only sources);
  NY DOH county PDFs (`health.ny.gov/.../by_resident_co/`) return 403 to curl — fetchable via Chrome pdf.js if a local number is wanted.

## 3. Vaccines and measles
- CRS IN12684 (updated July 29, 2026, `everycrsreport_com_reports_IN12684_html.txt`): June 9, 2025 the Secretary removed all 17
  ACIP members; COVID vaccine for children moved to shared decision-making; MMRV recommended against; **hepatitis B birth dose
  changed from universal to shared decision-making for infants of HBsAg-negative/unknown mothers (ACIP 8–3, Dec 5, 2025, NPR)**;
  Jan 2026 new childhood schedule issued without ACIP. **March 16, 2026: D. Mass. in AAP v. Kennedy stayed the 2026 schedule,
  the 13 appointments and all votes after June 11, 2025**, reverting mostly to the Jan 2025 schedules. May 19, 2026 new ACIP
  charter; May 29, 2026 EO 14407.
- CDC measles page, Sept 25, 2026 (`cdc-measles-*.txt`): 3,659 confirmed cases in 2026 through Sept 24 (2,289 in all of 2025;
  285 in 2024); 61% of 2026 cases under 20; 95% unvaccinated/unknown; 1 death in 2026, 3 in 2025; kindergarten MMR coverage
  95.2% (2019–20) → 92.4% (2025–26). PAHO RVC reviewed U.S. status April 13, 2026 (`paho_*.txt`); CIDRAP May 6, 2026: Lancet
  letter says U.S. "highly likely" to lose elimination at the November 2026 RVC meeting; ASTHO: the Americas region lost
  elimination in Nov 2025 via Canada. **Do not write that the U.S. has lost elimination status; as of retained sources it has not.**

## 4. HHS staffing cuts that hit child programs
- STAT Apr 4, 2025: CDC division handling asthma control and childhood lead poisoning prevention effectively eliminated in the
  April 1 RIF; NBC May 22, 2025: lead program "still defunct" despite Kennedy's Milwaukee claim; CNN June 11, 2025: HHS reinstated
  450+ CDC employees "including workers focused on HIV and childhood lead exposure" (158 at NCEH).
- K-12 Dive: April 1, 2025 RIF closed five Office of Head Start regional offices (Boston, New York, Chicago, San Francisco, Seattle).
  See also existing entry `2026-01-31-childcare-freeze.md`.
