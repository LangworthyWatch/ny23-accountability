# Beagle / animal-testing posts: recount from fresh captures, November 15, 2026

The site operator screenshotted every beagle and animal-testing post found on his official Facebook page (all show the page name "Congressman Nick Langworthy", the RepLangworthy page) on November 15, 2026, between 10:02 and 10:34 AM. There were 42 cropped captures; 3 are off topic and excluded (Nov 13 FAA post, H.R. 1013 retirement post, May 4 Ramsey's Playhouse opening, which also shows private individuals including apparent minors).

## Files
- `ledger.csv`: one row per capture (42 rows; 39 included). Columns: n, file, page_name, date_shown, date_iso, date_relative, text (verbatim, his spelling kept), format, linked_or_embedded, credit_or_claim, private_individuals, notes. Each date and caption was checked against the image, not only OCR.
- `captures/NN-YYYY-MM-DD.jpg`: 29 of the 39 included captures (JPEG-compressed).
- **Not committed:** 10 captures that show identifiable private individuals (03, 04, 06, 11, 20, 21, 24, 25, 39, 40). Their text is in the ledger. The originals are the operator's own screenshots (Desktop, "Screenshot 2026-11-15 at 10.MM.SS AM.png"); keep them.
