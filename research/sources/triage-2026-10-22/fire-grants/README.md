- Olean: $70,714 to CITY OF OLEAN, listed in his Sept 28, 2026 release ("through the FEMA AFG Grant Program"); not yet in USAspending (Oct 21 search). City Finance Committee agenda June 23, 2026 (raw/olean-finance-agenda-2026-06-23.txt): PL #65-26 authorizes an FY2025 AFG application "for the purchase of a radios."
- How AFG awards are chosen (FY2024 AFG notice, FR 2025-01303, raw/fr-2025-01303.txt): applications "electronically pre-scored and ranked" (50% of score), then "scored competitively by no less than three members of a Peer Review Panel" of "fire service representatives" (50%), plus FEMA technical review. Congress appropriated $324M for FY2024 AFG in P.L. 118-47.
