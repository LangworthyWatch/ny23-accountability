## The "public money" provisions (verbatim in enrolled text)
- CARES Sec. 2201: recovery rebate "$1,200 ($2,400 in the case of eligible individuals filing a joint return)"; Sec. 2104: "an additional amount of $600" weekly (Federal Pandemic Unemployment Compensation), through July 31, 2020.
- P.L. 116-260 Div. N Sec. 272: additional rebate "$600 ( $1,200 in the case of eligible individuals filing a joint return)"; Sec. 203: FPUC "$300" for weeks after Dec 26, 2020 through March 14, 2021.
- ARPA Sec. 9601: "$1,400 ($2,800 in the case of a joint return)"; Sec. 9013: FPUC extended to September 6, 2021.

## Assessment
Does not support a standalone MISSING CONTEXT entry:
- It is an opinion about effects, not a factual claim.
- He said "we," not "Democrats," so the record (much of the money signed by President Trump with broad Republican support; ARPA Democratic-only) does not contradict or reframe what he said.
- "Not to leave the house" refers to stay-at-home orders, which were state actions (in NY, Gov. Cuomo's executive orders), not the federal relief laws.
