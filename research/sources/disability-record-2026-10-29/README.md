## B. Recorded votes
- H.R. 1 / OBBBA: Aye RC 145 (May 22, 2025) and RC 190 (July 3, 2025). Disability-relevant provisions
  (NHeLP webinar deck, nhelp-obbba-pwd-webinar.txt, Oct 23, 2025): Medicaid work requirements for
  ages 19-64 from Jan 1, 2027 (exemptions on paper for "medically frail," HCBS waiver participants,
  caregivers of a disabled person, veterans with total disability); six-month redeterminations from
  Jan 1, 2027 ("increases risk of procedural terminations... burden on people with disabilities");
  retroactive coverage shortened; cost sharing up to $35/service from Oct 1, 2028; provider-tax freeze
  ("will impact HCBS funding"); nursing-home staffing-rule moratorium rider (home-care entry). CBO:
  10M uninsured by 2034 incl. 1.3M duals; SNAP ~$186B. AAPD: "This Is A Devastating Day for Disabled
  Americans." Fair: the law also created a new HCBS waiver option with some state funding (NHeLP).
- H.R. 9237 (Rules, June 2026): Record Vote 369 Nay (keeping off the floor the amendment to pass the
  Major Richard Star Act without the sleep-apnea/tinnitus rating cuts), Vote 373 Yea (closed rule).
  Existing ratings protected; ~$57B/10yr per VA analysis cited by DAV/VFW/IAVA. Entry 2026-07-03.
- FY2026 omnibus: Yea RC 45 (Jan 22) and RC 53 (Feb 3, 2026), funded IDEA and OCR roughly flat (fair).
- H.R. 82 Social Security Fairness Act: Yea, RC 456, Nov 12, 2024, 327-75 (fair).

   (5:24-cv-00225) vacated HHS's 504 integration provisions on a joint DOJ/plaintiff motion
   (leadingage-504-vacated-2026-09.txt; npr-2026-10-01). This is what H.R. 9401 answers. CDR's Darling:
   "like a knife in your chest." S.Res. 790 (June 24) and a Dingell draft H.Res. (Aug 5) call for
   rescission; no House number located for the Dingell text (hres 1480-1530 scanned).
2. SSI "public assistance household" rescission, RIN 0960-AI94: listed in the Spring 2025 Unified
   Agenda (NPRM target 08/2025), absent from later agendas (reginfo 202510/202604 show "Spring 2025"
   only); no proposed rule in the Federal Register as of 2026-10-29 (FR API search; positive control:
   an Aug 31, 2026 SSA ISM notice surfaced). ProPublica (Eli Hager, Apr 28, 2026): still being pushed
   by White House/DOGE officials, up to 400,000 SSI recipients, cuts up to about a third (~$330/mo in
   the profiled case). Already in the May 18 AAPD entry.
3. Education Dept. interagency agreement, June 16, 2026: HHS to administer IDEA and Rehab Act grants,
