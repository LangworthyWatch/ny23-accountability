Title: Inflation stretching N.Y. disability service agencies thin
URL: https://spectrumlocalnews.com/nys/central-ny/news/2025/08/21/inflation-stretches-disability-service-agencies-thin
BY BRIANNE ROESSER NEW YORK STATE
PUBLISHED 5:02 PM ET AUG. 21, 2025
(retained from browser page text, 2026-10-29)

Agencies across New York that support people with intellectual and developmental disabilities said they're feeling the squeeze of inflation.

Suburban Adult Services, Inc. (SASI) serves nearly 2,000 people across the state. Their president and CEO, Jeff Paterson, said increases to operational costs like utilities and supplies alone are stretching their budget by more than $10,000.

The direct support professionals who work for these agencies have been advocating for pay raises for years, and now with inflation, Paterson said their dollars don't go as far.

"That's money that you can't spend on your staff, that you can't spend on the quality-of-life things for the people that you support," said Paterson. "It might mean that you can't go out and do as many things in the community."

Paterson said on top of inflation, cuts to SNAP and Medicaid are also an issue.

The key to staying afloat, he said, is collaboration with other support agencies across the state, and advocacy at the local, state and federal levels.
