In its latest notice of additional store closures, filed last week with the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of New Jersey, theembattled pharmacy chain announced an additional 114 locations that had been marked for closure.
Why It Matters
In May, Rite Aid announced that it would again be entering the Chapter 11 process, having done so in October 2023, and that itwould be selling off and closing its roughly 1,200 locations as a result. Prior to the most recent filing, Rite Aid had alreadyannounced more than 1,000 store closures as of late June.

What Happens Next
It is unclear when the stores will shutter for good, though employees at certain locations in Pennsylvania and Connecticut havetold local media that they expect to close in August.
Many of Rite Aid's locations and pharmacy assets, including prescription files, have already secured buyers. Late last month, thecompany received court approval to sell its Thrifty Ice Cream brand for $19 million.
