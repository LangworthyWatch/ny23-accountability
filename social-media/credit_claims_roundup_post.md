Seven times this year, Rep. Langworthy told NY-23 he "secured," "delivered" or did something. Each time, the record tells a different story about who did the work, or whether it's done.

CLAIM: "he has secured $5,000,000" for the Newstead Community Center
VERDICT: MISSING CONTEXT
Sens. Schumer and Gillibrand announced the same $5 million for the same building the same day. He didn't mention them.

CLAIM: "I was proud to deliver $300,000" for a hospital X-ray lab
VERDICT: MISLEADING
It's a 2027 funding request. It isn't in any bill that has become law.

CLAIM: "I secured $1 million" for the Olean Police Department
VERDICT: DOCUMENTED PATTERN
It passed the House. The Senate hasn't acted.

CLAIM: "SECURED $1,250,000" for Allegany County water
VERDICT: DOCUMENTED PATTERN
At least the third round of announcements for the same earmark. None mentioned that Sens. Schumer and Gillibrand also requested it and announced it first.

CLAIM: "My Dairy Farm Resiliency Act strengthened the Dairy Margin Coverage program."
VERDICT: MISLEADING
His bill never got a hearing or a vote. The language was first in another member's 2023 bill, and became law through the committee chairman's farm-bill text.

CLAIM: "I'm proud to secure $1.4 million for Chemung County"
VERDICT: MISSING CONTEXT
It's an Appalachian Regional Commission grant. The county applied, and ARC decided.

CLAIM: "we took swift and decisive action against 50 high-risk Medicaid providers"
VERDICT: MISLEADING
CMS, HHS's inspector general and state agencies took the action. His committee's role was oversight, and he isn't on the subcommittee that held the fraud hearings.

The fair reading: most of this money and enforcement is real, and four of these came from earmark requests his office made. That's real work. "Requested," or "helped secure, with the senators," would have been accurate. Senators sometimes leave him out of their announcements, too.
