Two in five households in NY-23 receive Social Security. In New York State and the nation, it's one in three.

That's from our new profile of the district on its current lines, built from Census and federal data:

→ Social Security supplies 10.5% of all household income here, against 6.3% statewide.
→ Nearly 1 in 4 residents is 65 or older, the highest estimate among New York's 26 districts (NY-3 is statistically tied). 1 in 4 has Medicare.
→ 1 in 8 working residents is in manufacturing, more than twice the state share.
→ Unemployment was 3.7% in 2025, below the state's 5.6%.
→ Median household income is $78,436, about $10,800 below the state's.
→ The places wholly inside the district lost about 12,100 people from 2020 to 2025.

When Congress votes on Social Security, Medicare or trade, these are the households those votes reach.
