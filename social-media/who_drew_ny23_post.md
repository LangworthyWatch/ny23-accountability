Is NY-23 gerrymandered? On the record, no. And Rep. Langworthy's description of the 2022 map holds up.

CLAIM: "the courts put fair maps on the table, that we ran on"
VERDICT: TRUE
REALITY: A court-appointed expert drew the 2022 map without regard to where incumbents lived. The judge called it "almost perfectly neutral." That version of NY-23 still voted for Trump by 17 points in 2020.

CLAIM: Albany's 2024 map is "a gerrymander that's led to them taking two seats"
VERDICT: MISSING CONTEXT
REALITY: It moved NY-3 and NY-22 toward Democrats, and they won both. But NY-3 was already won on the court's lines, the NY-22 winner's margin was more than double the shift, and no court has found the 2024 map to be a gerrymander.

→ The 2024 map also moved Pendleton, his hometown, into NY-23. On the court's map he lived in NY-26.

Overall: MOSTLY TRUE.
