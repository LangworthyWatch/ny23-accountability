James Comer chairs Oversight. He sits on both.

The fraud documents his committee and CMS published this year, and his 70 press releases since August 2025, contain no mention of nursing homes. His one recorded nursing-home action is a vote for the law that blocked the federal staffing rule.

What is ordinary here: joint fundraising committees are a routine tool, LLC gifts are lawful when attributed to their owners, the filings were made on time, and $31,386 to his campaign and PAC is small next to the $2.3 million he has raised. Two of the six homes are four-star. No link to any vote is claimed.

What the record shows is the shape: three members whose committees oversee nursing homes, six nursing-home donors, one check-writing day, eleven weeks. He has taken $55,000 from another nursing-home operator before; that entry is linked, and so is every filing.
