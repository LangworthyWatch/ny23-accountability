Eighteen months after promising to "absolutely lead the fight" to fully fund air traffic control, Rep. Langworthy has not introduced a bill to do it.

He did call for full funding back then, and he voted for the money that reached the floor: $12.5 billion in H.R. 1 and $4 billion in the 2026 spending law. Here is the rest of the record:

→ No air traffic control bill of his own. His aviation bills cover charter flight screening and TSA pay.
→ No seat on either committee that writes FAA law or funding.
→ 1 of his 455 press releases since 2023 is about air traffic control.

On November 17 he stood with crash victims' families and said aviation safety "deserves constant attention, not just when tragedy strikes." The administration now wants $30.1 billion to $50 billion more from Congress.
