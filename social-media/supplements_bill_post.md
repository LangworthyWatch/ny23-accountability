Rep. Langworthy says his supplements bill will make them "safe, effective, accessible and affordable." The bill has no safety or effectiveness provision.

CLAIM: "My bill creates a national regulatory framework"
VERDICT: MISSING CONTEXT
REALITY: It makes federal rules the only rules. A state would need HHS approval to keep a stricter one, like New York's ban on sales to minors. It adds no new federal rule.

CLAIM: It will "ensure vitamins and supplements are safe, effective"
VERDICT: NOT SUPPORTED
REALITY: FDA can't approve supplements as safe or effective before sale. The bill doesn't change that.

→ New York and Oregon have gone after mislabeled or spiked products already on sale, and California's health department counted 776 products FDA found with hidden drugs.
→ 12 groups lobbied on the bill this year, including all four of its endorsers. One gave his campaign $5,000.

In his favor: one national rule is a real policy goal, and the bill got a committee hearing in April.
