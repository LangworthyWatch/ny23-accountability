Rep. Langworthy says his supplements bill will make them "safe, effective." The bill has no safety or effectiveness provision.

CLAIM: It will "ensure vitamins and supplements are safe, effective"
VERDICT: NOT SUPPORTED
REALITY: FDA says it "does NOT have the authority to approve dietary supplements for safety and effectiveness" before sale. The bill leaves that unchanged.

CLAIM: "My bill creates a national regulatory framework"
VERDICT: MISSING CONTEXT
REALITY: It makes federal rules the only rules. New York's ban on selling weight-loss and muscle-building supplements to minors would be overridden unless HHS granted an exemption.

→ The Natural Products Association sued New York in 2023 to strike down that law. The suit was dismissed. It endorsed his bill the day he introduced it, and its PAC gave his campaign $5,000 in March. No exchange is alleged.

In his favor: one national rule is a real policy goal.
