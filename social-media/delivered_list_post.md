He listed ten things he is "proud to have delivered." Six of them are agency grants that no member of Congress directs.

We have now checked all ten. Every verdict below is from a published entry, and we start with what held up.

TWO HELD UP, AND WE SAY SO PLAINLY:
→ $60.5M Jasper-Troupsburg School Campus: Mostly true. The FEMA award is real, his advocacy is documented, and the superintendent credits him by name.
→ $480K Elmira College: Accurate. His own earmark, requested solo, legitimately secured.

TWO ARE EARMARKS HE SHARES CREDIT FOR, WITHOUT SAYING SO:
→ $5M Newstead Community Center: Missing context. Senators Schumer and Gillibrand announced the identical $5 million the same day. One appropriation, not two.
→ $1.25M Allegany County water: Documented pattern. A joint request with the senators, announced by him a third time, still without mentioning them.

SIX ARE AGENCY AWARDS, WON BY THE RECIPIENTS:
→ $5.7M Jamestown Airport and $3.1M Southern Tier airports: FAA formula grants. His own release names the program. Documented pattern.
→ $7.7M early childhood and $1.4M Schuyler Head Start: HHS awards. His own release says Schuyler Head Start "has been awarded" the grant. Documented pattern.
→ $3.2M Pomfret water: agency grant. Documented pattern.
→ $1M Chautauqua Mental Hygiene: the county won a competitive SAMHSA grant by applying. A member does not pick the winner. Missing context.

His framing is fair and we will quote it: "I want Washington spending less. But once the pie is set in stone, I'm fighting to bring home as many resources as possible to NY23." Announcing federal grants is a normal part of the job.

But "announced" is what his own press releases say. "Delivered" is what the list says. Of ten items, two are his without an asterisk.

Don't take our word for it. All six entries, with the receipts, are on the site.
