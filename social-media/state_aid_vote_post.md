Upstate, the counties that voted most heavily for Trump get the most state aid per resident. NY-23's counties are near the top.

→ In fiscal 2024, Cattaraugus County received more state aid per resident than any other county outside New York City. Allegany ranked fourth and Steuben seventh.
→ NY-23's seven whole counties averaged about $3,568 per resident, against $2,498 for the rest of upstate.
→ Aid formulas don't look at votes. State school aid, about three-quarters of the total, is written to send more to districts with less property wealth and income. NY-23's counties have about half the property value per resident of the rest of upstate.
→ Statewide, counting New York City and federal aid too, counties Trump won and counties Harris won come out about even: $3,325 and $3,404 per resident.

Worth knowing the next time the question of who gets what from Albany comes up.

Data and method: The Public Ledgers, from State Comptroller filings.
