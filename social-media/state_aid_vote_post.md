Upstate counties that Trump won by 20 or more points get about 43% more state aid per resident than the rest of upstate. Six of NY-23's seven counties are in that group.

→ Fiscal 2024 state aid to local governments, per resident: $3,385 in those 22 counties, $2,385 in the counties Harris won, and $2,343 in the counties Trump won by less.
→ The difference tracks property wealth. Aid formulas don't look at votes. School aid, about three-quarters of the total, sends more to places with less to tax. The 20-point counties average $84,000 in property per resident; the counties Trump won narrowly, including Long Island's suburbs, average $207,000.
→ Cattaraugus County gets more state aid per resident than any other county outside New York City.
→ Statewide, counting New York City and federal aid too, counties Trump won and counties Harris won come out about even.

Worth knowing the next time the question of who gets what from Albany comes up.

Data and method: The Public Ledgers, from State Comptroller filings.
