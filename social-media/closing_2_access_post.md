Day two of the record. Who can reach him, and who funds him. Every number is from his own filings and the committee's own vote sheets.

REACHING HIM
→ No in-person public town hall since he took office in January 2023.
→ 26 of 26 constituent letters we have documented were answered with form letters. On March 11, 2026, two different constituents writing on two different topics got replies stamped 12:44 PM.
→ Telephone town halls: same-day notice, recurring postponements, selective recordings.

FUNDING HIM
→ $2,199,540 raised this cycle through September 30. $1,201,227 of it, 55 percent, from PACs and other political committees.
→ Matching every itemized donor's ZIP code to the district: at most 17 cents of every dollar came from someone with an address in NY-23. Counting only ZIP codes entirely inside the district, 9 cents.
→ Donors outside New York gave $259,013. ZIP codes wholly inside NY-23 gave $194,775.

SHOWING UP
→ He says he takes "the 'Oversight' in Oversight Committee seriously." The committee's vote sheets record him absent for 69 of its 145 markup roll calls this Congress.
→ He did not attend the committee's February 2026 deposition of Les Wexner, Epstein's most prominent financial patron.

The fair reading: PAC-heavy fundraising is common for incumbents in safe seats, and no filing shows a donor changing a vote. The site does not claim one did. The question is simpler: when did a constituent last get to ask him something in a room?
