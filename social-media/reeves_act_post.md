→ The bill was introduced June 23, five days after the Justice Department said federal law does not require states to serve disabled people "in the most integrated setting." → We found no statement by him on the DOJ opinion or the court order. Not in 70 press releases, not on X, not anywhere we searched.
→ In 2025 he voted twice for the law that cuts Medicaid by $840 billion, per CBO. Medicaid pays for the home- and community-based services the Reeves Act is meant to protect. The disability community's largest advocacy group called the day it passed "a devastating day for disabled Americans."
→ He is still not on the IDEA Full Funding Act for special education. Three fellow New York Republicans are. It has waited 18 months.

What cuts the other way: the cosponsorship is real, and the advocates were right to welcome it. He also joined an SSI bill on October 1, signed a 2023 letter against home-health cuts, and is not on the bill to abolish the Education Department.
