This one holds up. Pell Grants now cover approved 8-to-14-week training programs, as Rep. Langworthy posted on October 27.

VERDICT: MOSTLY TRUE

→ The July 2025 law created Workforce Pell Grants for programs of 150 to 599 clock hours over 8 to 14 weeks, starting July 1, 2026. The governor and the Education Department both have to approve each program.
→ He voted for that law twice.
→ The one catch is spoken, not written. In the video he says Pell "used to be only associates." It didn't. Training programs of 600 or more hours were already eligible with no degree required. What's new is coverage for the short ones.
