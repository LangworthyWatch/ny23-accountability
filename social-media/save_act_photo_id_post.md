The SAVE Act does not ask for a photo ID. It asks for proof of citizenship, handed over in person.

Rep. Langworthy posted this week that 83% of Americans support showing identification to vote. That number is real, and we said so back in February.

CLAIM: "Recent polling showed 83% of Americans support showing identification to vote."
VERDICT: True, but missing context
Pew (Aug. 4 to 10, 2025, n=3,554) found 83% back a government photo ID. Republicans 95%, Democrats 71%. The newest poll, Fox News (July 17 to 20, 2026), splits the question: 83% to register, 77% to cast a ballot. Voter ID is genuinely popular either way.

CLAIM: That the SAVE Act is that photo ID requirement.
VERDICT: Misleading
What H.R. 22 actually requires:
→ Documentary proof of citizenship, presented IN PERSON to an election office. That reaches mail registration, and the registration drives built on it.
→ A standard REAL ID does not qualify. It counts only if it indicates citizenship, and most do not.
→ Otherwise: a passport, or a photo ID paired with a certified birth certificate meeting seven conditions.
→ The words "married," "maiden," and "marriage" appear nowhere in the bill. It hands the name-mismatch problem to the states with no federal standard for cost or timing.

What compliance costs in NY-23:
→ NY birth certificate by mail: $30, and 10 to 12 weeks processing
→ Expedited online or by phone: $45
→ First-time adult passport: $165

Two points in his favor, and they belong here:
→ New York is one of a handful of states issuing an Enhanced License that is REAL ID compliant AND establishes citizenship. Many NY-23 residents already hold one. Most Americans have no such option.
→ The bill does include a fallback. An applicant can submit other evidence, with an official signing an affidavit. That is real, and critics often leave it out. It is also discretionary and has no track record.

We are not saying this bill would stop any particular person from voting. We are saying that "showing identification" is not what it asks for.

Don't take our word for it. Check the bill text and his own post.
