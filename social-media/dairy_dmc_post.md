"My Dairy Farm Resiliency Act strengthened the Dairy Margin Coverage program." His bill never got a hearing.

On Oct 7 his official page posted that his Dairy Farm Resiliency Act strengthened the dairy safety net and "protects up to 6 million pounds of milk." The 6-million-pound change is real. We checked whose bill did it.

CLAIM: His Dairy Farm Resiliency Act strengthened the Dairy Margin Coverage program.
VERDICT: MISLEADING

→ H.R. 294 was referred to subcommittee Feb 14, 2025. No hearing, no markup, no vote since. The law that changed the program lists 29 related bills; his is not one of them.
→ The bill was Rep. Marc Molinaro's first: same name, same text, filed June 2023. Langworthy was not a cosponsor. He refiled it under his own name after Molinaro lost his seat.
→ The 6-million-pound language was already in the House Agriculture Committee's own farm bill in May 2024, eight months before he filed. The committee ordered it reported 33 to 21.
→ What became law (One Big Beautiful Bill Act, Sec. 10313) tracks the committee's bill line by line: 6 million pounds, 2021-2023 production history, 25% discount, extended to 2031.
→ His bill's one original idea, resetting production history every five years, was not enacted.
→ The dairy lobby's statements credit Chairman GT Thompson and the committee. Neither names him or his bill.

The fair reading: he sat on the Agriculture Committee when it adopted the text, his bill contains the same line, and he voted for the law twice. "Protects up to 6 million pounds when prices drop or feed costs soar" is an accurate description of the program. He can say he supported the change. He cannot say his Act did it.

His own January 2025 release described this bill only as a five-year production-history update, the part that did not become law, and never mentioned 6 million pounds. The October 2026 release describes it only by the 6-million-pound change, the part that did.

Every quote is from the bill texts, the status files, USDA and his own releases, cited in the entry.
