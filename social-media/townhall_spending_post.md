Digging into Congressman Nick Langworthy's office expenses, your tax dollars: one advertising buy ($150,000) cost his office more than twenty tele-town halls ($7,416 each, his own filings).

We pulled thirteen quarters of his office's own spending records, January 2023 through March 2026, and published the whole dataset.

TALKING AT YOU: $623,882 on advertising, 78 payments
WHERE YOU TALK BACK: $281,795 on tele-town halls, 38 events
VERDICT: Documented pattern

→ A single $150,000 payment to one ad firm, for spots that ran Aug. 15 to Sep. 2, 2024. At the average cost of a tele-town hall, that one payment buys twenty of them. He has held 38 in three years.
→ Two quarters account for 79% of all advertising. Four quarters came in under $2,000.
→ Zero tele-town halls billed in Oct-Dec 2024, the quarter containing the election.
→ No in-person town halls since January 2023. The tele-town hall is the only standing public Q&A there is.

Three things we checked and did NOT find, because you deserve to know what the record does not say:

→ No franking violation. The pre-election blackout runs 60 days and covers any digital advertisement regardless of cost. For Nov. 5, 2024 it opened Sept. 6. Every large buy ran before it, the closest by three days.
→ No shared vendors between the official office and the campaign. We checked all four advertising vendors against his campaign committee's full FEC history. Zero payments, ever.
→ We are not saying any single call was overpriced. A tele-town hall dials tens of thousands of households at once. His own follow-up post said "tens of thousands tuned in," which works out to roughly 25 cents per constituent reached, cheaper per contact than mail.

The point is not the price of a phone call. It is that the one channel where you can ask him a question directly gets 45 cents for every dollar spent on the channel that talks at you.

Every figure comes from his office's own disbursement filings. The full dataset is published so you can check it line by line.
