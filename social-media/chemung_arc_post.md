On Facebook, Rep. Langworthy says he's "proud to secure $1.4 million" for Chemung County's sewers. His own press release says he "announced" a grant from the Appalachian Regional Commission. The post never mentions the agency.

The grant is real, and it's good news: $1.4 million from ARC plus $1.4 million in state money for a $2.8 million sewer upgrade at the Southern Tier Logistics Business Park in Horseheads. We checked what "secure" means here.

VERDICT: MISSING CONTEXT

→ Sen. Schumer announced the same grant the same day and says the money comes from the 2021 infrastructure law, passed before Rep. Langworthy took office.
→ His release describes no role for him in winning the grant. The regional planning board says support letters from members of Congress are "nice to have." We found none from him, though such letters are usually not public.
