The Pentagon's public death count for the Iran war went down last week. From 18 to 14. One of the names that came off belonged to Sgt. Angel Rampersad, a New Yorker.

Gov. Hochul: "Changing a website does not rewrite the truth of her sacrifice."

The Pentagon calls it a technical fault. What is not in dispute: the administration told Congress on May 1 that the operation was "terminated." Strikes since are being called "overseas operations." Rep. Thomas Massie, a Republican, calls that an "absurd ruse" to dodge the 60-day war powers clock. The same relabeling that stops the clock also decides which war a casualty gets counted under.

Nick Langworthy sits on the House Oversight Committee, in the majority. It is the committee that investigates federal agencies. He could request a hearing. We found none, and no statement.

The rest of the record:

→ 7 votes to keep an unauthorized war going. Two of those measures passed the House anyway. One failed on a 212 to 212 tie.

→ More than 10,000 veterans lost their homes after VA ended its mortgage rescue program in May 2025. The mortgage industry had told a House committee two months earlier exactly what would happen: "Foreclosure. Period." Congress authorized a replacement that July. VA did not open it until June 15, 2026. The Mortgage Bankers Association puts VA foreclosures at their highest since 2017.

→ Sleep apnea pays a 50% rating today for a veteran who needs a CPAP. Under the bill he voted 7 times to protect, future claims get 0% if treatment works. Existing ratings are safe. The people who pay are deployed right now.

→ 1,102 fewer VBA claims examiners, 67,849 claims already past 125 days.

→ VA research staffing down 5.1% in the FY2027 request. Research is how the next PACT Act ever gets proven.

What cuts the other way: he voted to RAISE toxic exposure funding, and for the bill that put VA research above last year. He has no PACT Act votes at all, that law passed before he took office. And he has no vote on the mortgage program either, it passed both chambers by voice vote.

Don't take our word for it. Every figure traces to a roll call, an IG report, or a VA filing you can open.
