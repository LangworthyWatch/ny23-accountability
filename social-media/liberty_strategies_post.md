We checked two claims on Congressman Langworthy's financial disclosures. One is true. The other cannot be checked by anyone, including us.

CLAIM: "I don't trade stocks, I've never traded stocks since I've been in public office."
VERDICT: True
→ Zero Periodic Transaction Reports in four years. The only transaction on his filings is a July 2024 rollover of an old employer 401(k) into his federal Thrift Savings Plan. His holdings are index funds and TSP. We checked because the rules let anyone check, and he checks out. We say so plainly.

THE OTHER HALF: spouse consulting income from a firm called Liberty Strategies.
VERDICT: Missing context
→ It appears on 4 of his 5 House disclosures as a single line.
→ The amount, every year: N/A. That is not concealment. The House form asks for the source of a spouse's earned income and never the amount.
→ Through 2022 the firm's work was visible, because its clients were campaigns that file their own reports: $103,603.99 from three New York Republican House committees, 2019 to 2022.
→ For 2023, 2024 and 2025 we located no payments to the firm in FEC records or the NYS Board of Elections database. The income line still appears on the 2024 and 2025 filings.

What we are NOT saying, and it matters:
→ No wrongdoing is alleged, and none was found.
→ A spouse having a career, including running a consulting business, is normal and lawful.
→ Private clients would not show up in campaign-finance databases at all. That is an ordinary explanation, and we can neither rule it in nor out.
→ Reporting the source without the amount is exactly what the rules require.

This is not about his family. It is that Congress wrote rules making stock trading checkable and did not write them here. Where verification is possible, his record verifies clean. Where it is not, nobody can form a view in either direction.
