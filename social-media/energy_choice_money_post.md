The trade group that says it "helped draft" Rep. Langworthy's Energy Choice Act has made him its top PAC recipient this cycle.

His campaign posted this month that the bill protects families' "freedom to choose how they heat their homes." Here is who backs it with money, from the FEC filings.

→ The National Energy & Fuels Institute (NEFI), a heating-fuel dealers' group, says the bill is one "which NEFI helped draft and has led a national coalition to support." Its PAC has paid his campaign $9,263 in 2025-26, more than it has paid any other committee.

→ His own June 2025 press release listed 17 national groups backing the bill. Six of them have PACs that gave his campaign and leadership PAC a combined $54,000: home builders $20,000, propane dealers $10,000, NEFI $8,500, the American Gas Association $7,500, plumbing-heating contractors $5,000, Energy Marketers of America $3,000.

→ The home builders' $10,000 arrived Nov 26, 2025, one week after a subcommittee advanced the bill and one week before the full committee voted 24 to 21 to send it to the House.

→ Oil, gas and propane PACs overall have given his committees $83,500 this cycle.

→ NEFI's year-end report also lists a $763.12 payment to his campaign for "Breakfast expenses." We could not find it in the campaign's own reports, and the filings don't say what it was.

The fair reading: all of this is legal and disclosed. PACs often give to members who already agree with them, and the records can't show which came first. The propane group sent identical checks to other members on the same days. NEFI's PAC also funds Democrats. And the $54,000 is about 4% of the PAC and party money his committees took in. This is a documented sequence, not proof of a deal.

But when a bill is sold as protecting your choices, you should know who says it helped write it and who funds its sponsor.

Don't take our word for it. Every figure comes from the FEC filings, linked in the entry.
