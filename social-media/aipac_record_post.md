# Facebook post: AIPAC and Langworthy, what the records show (July 25, 2026)
Companion to aipac_record_card.png and the fact-check.

---

Langworthy didn't just vote for the Israel arms bill. He wrote the rule that put it on the floor, and that rule barred amendments.

People ask about him and AIPAC constantly. We pulled the primary records: FEC filings, House Clerk roll calls, GPO sponsor rosters, his travel disclosure. Four claims, checked.

CLAIM: AIPAC's super PAC spent money to elect him.
VERDICT: FALSE
Zero independent expenditures for or against him, from any committee, in any cycle. United Democracy Project has never spent a dollar on his race.

CLAIM: AIPAC is one of his biggest donors.
VERDICT: MISSING CONTEXT
AIPAC's PAC wrote him $25,000. That's ordinary: 23 other groups gave him exactly $10,000. But AIPAC also bundles, collecting and forwarding checks from individual donors. That's another $76,016 across 75 contributions. Total routed through AIPAC: $101,016.

CLAIM: He took an AIPAC-funded trip to Israel.
VERDICT: TRUE
April 1 to 8, 2024. Buffalo to Israel and back, $15,900.02, paid by AIPAC's charitable affiliate. Properly disclosed on both required filings. It was not his first visit.

CLAIM: He votes on Israel like any Republican would.
VERDICT: MISSING CONTEXT
He sponsored and floor-managed H.Res. 1227, the closed rule for the Israel arms bill. Yes on Israel aid and No on Ukraine aid the same day, April 20, 2024. No on all six Iran war powers resolutions, two of which passed anyway.

Two things we will not say, because the records don't support them. He did not skip the Antisemitism Awareness Act vote on purpose: he missed every vote that day. And the quote about visiting destroyed Israeli communities that gets pinned on him belongs to a different member of Congress.

Bundled fundraising, advocacy money, and Ethics-approved travel are all legal. We are documenting the record, not alleging a deal.

Don't take our word for it. Every figure traces to a filing you can open yourself.
