In May 2025, Rep. Langworthy said he would "absolutely lead the fight" to fully fund air traffic control upgrades. After the November 13 nationwide ground stop, he says he has "long called for" them. He did call for it. Here is what the record shows since.

→ He voted for the $12.5 billion in H.R. 1, the $4 billion in the 2026 spending law, and the ROTOR aviation safety bill, which failed by one vote.
→ He has introduced no bill to fund or fix air traffic control. His aviation bills cover charter flight screening and TSA pay.
→ He sits on neither committee that writes FAA law or funding.
→ He signed one letter asking appropriators to have the FAA fix its most at-risk facilities first. Another member led it.

The administration is asking Congress for $30.1 billion to $50 billion more. On November 16 he said he hoped the House would have "a very serious discussion."
