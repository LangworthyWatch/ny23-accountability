## Why this matters

Most of what this site checks is a claim that turns out to be missing
something. This one mostly holds up, and it is worth saying so plainly.
Rep. Langworthy's post lists four positions of the Democratic Socialists of
America. Three appear in DSA's own platform in nearly those words and the
fourth is what DSA's 2025 platform says, as quoted by multiple outlets. The
gap is one word, "illegal," and the context is what the resolution he voted
for does and does not do.

---

## Statement

**Source:** Facebook, official office page ("Congressman Nick Langworthy"),
September 4, 2026, with a clip of his remarks at the House Rules Committee
meeting of August 31:

> "The ugly weed of Socialism planted itself firmly in New York State. It is
> now our duty to stop it from spreading further across America. The DSA's
> radical agenda: let illegal aliens vote, abolish the Senate, close prisons,
> and let Congress, not the American people, elect the President. These
> anti-American radicals must be stopped."

In the clip he says DSA members are "openly advocating allowing
non-citizens to vote in American elections," and House Administration
Chairman Bryan Steil, whom he invites to respond, adds that DSA wants to
"abolish the system in which we elect the President, allow Congress to
elect it," and "abolish the Senate."

## The record

### The resolution

H.Res. 1490, "Providing for the condemnation and denouncement of socialism
in all its forms," was reported by the Rules Committee 7–4 on August 31 and
passed the House on September 1, **220–192**, with two voting present (Roll
Call 289; Langworthy Yea; eight Democrats voted yes). Its operative clauses
condemn "socialism in all its forms, including the Democratic Socialists of
America," reaffirm support for secure elections, state that "American
elections are for American citizens only," and call for enactment of the
SAVE America Act. Nowhere does it define socialism; see the note below.

### The four planks, against DSA's own words

| His words | What DSA says | Source | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| "let illegal aliens vote" | 2021 platform: "voting rights and a path to citizenship for every US resident." DSA's Feb. 19, 2026 statement: the system treats "its residents—citizens and noncitizens alike—as subjects, rather than full political participants." | DSA platform (archived); DSA statement (archived) | **Substantially true.** DSA's language is "every US resident" and "noncitizens," which does not carve out immigration status; "illegal aliens" is his gloss, not their phrasing |
| "abolish the Senate" | "End minority rule. Abolish the Senate and the Electoral College." | 2021 platform, verbatim | **True** |
| "close prisons" | "Decarceration and eventual abolition of the carceral state." | 2021 platform, verbatim | **True in substance**; DSA's word is "eventual," his is "close" |
| "let Congress, not the American people, elect the President" | The 2025 platform would "replace the president and Supreme Court with an executive judiciary chosen by and subordinate to Congress," with the Senate abolished and the House expanded. | CNN, Aug. 3, 2026, quoting the platform; Washington Examiner, July 2026, same quotation | **True** as reported; the platform text itself could not be retrieved (see disclosure) |

**In plain language:** on what DSA wants, the post is accurate. The one
stretch is "illegal aliens," where DSA's actual language is "every US
resident" and "noncitizens"; that covers people without status, but it is
not what they wrote, and the resolution's own whereas clause uses
"noncitizens."

## The context

- **The resolution never defines what it condemns.** H.Res. 1490 condemns
  "socialism in all its forms" and "socialist policies" without saying what
  either is; its whereas clauses describe consequences ("collapsed into
  communist regimes," "famine and mass murders") and DSA planks, not a
  definition. Three of its five operative clauses are about election rules,
  and the fifth calls for a specific bill. It is a messaging vote, and it
  passed as one.
- **"Planted itself firmly in New York State."** The resolution's own
  factual anchor is the November 2025 New York City mayoral election and DSA
  candidates running "in over one hundred elections in at least half the
  states." This site's [June entry](/fact-checks/2026-06-30-mamdani-primaries-socialist-claim/)
  on his Mamdani post found the "socialist" label fair, "communist" false,
  and the "across NYS" claim overstated; nothing here changes that.
- **His floor statement was narrower than his post.** His September 1
  release calls socialism "a corrosive ideology spreading through the
  Democrat Party" and says "I will always stand on this side of individual
  liberty and Constitutional freedom." The post adds the four planks, which
  is where it becomes checkable, and where it holds.

## Questions this raises

1. The resolution condemns "socialist policies" without defining them.
   Does the Congressman consider Medicare, Social Security, rural electric
   cooperatives, or the Rural Health Transformation Program he supports to
   fall inside or outside that term?
2. DSA's platform language is "every US resident." Why does the post say
   "illegal aliens" rather than "noncitizens," the word the resolution
   itself uses?
